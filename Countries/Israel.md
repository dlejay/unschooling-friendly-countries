# Unschooling in Israel

## Compulsory education

Under the Compulsory Education Law, education is compulsory from age 3 through the end of 12th grade.

Parents are ordinarily required to enrol their child in, and ensure attendance at, a recognised educational institution.

## Home education

Parents may apply to the Ministry of Education for an exemption from compulsory attendance in order to educate their child at home.

The Ministry's policy is that children of compulsory-school age should ordinarily attend recognised educational institutions. An exemption for home education may be granted where the parents present a coherent educational worldview that rejects education within a school institution, or where exceptional circumstances justify home education.

Applications for the following school year must normally be submitted between 1 December and 30 April through the Ministry of Education's Parent Portal, with a copy sent to the education department of the relevant local authority.

Until an exemption has been granted, the child must continue to attend a recognised educational institution.

The application must include, among other documents:

- a statement setting out the parents' educational worldview;
- a detailed educational programme;
- details of the adult responsible for the child's education;
- a daily and weekly schedule; and
- methods for assessing the child's progress.

The educational programme must cover at least 55% of the knowledge areas included in the Ministry of Education's core curriculum for the child's age or level of functioning. It must also be designed so that the child could subsequently enter or return to a recognised educational institution at an appropriate educational level.

Before the application is considered, a Ministry representative normally conducts a home visit to meet the child and parents and examine the proposed educational environment. The application is then considered by a district home-education committee, with the final decision made by the district director.

An exemption is generally granted for one school year. The district director may grant it for a shorter or longer period, but not for more than two consecutive school years. A new application is required when the exemption expires.

Parents must report on the child's progress and compliance with the approved programme. The Ministry may also conduct further home visits during the exemption period.

The home-education procedure requires the child to reside in Israel. Compulsory education also applies to foreign children residing in Israel irrespective of their immigration status; Ministry guidance generally applies the obligation to foreign children after they have lived in Israel for more than three months.
