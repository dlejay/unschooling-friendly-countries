Papua New Guinea's 22 province-level divisions include the National Capital District and the Autonomous Region of Bougainville. Bougainville has a separate education law and is assessed separately. [DFAT Country Information Report, 25 July 2025, paragraph 2.1](https://www.dfat.gov.au/sites/default/files/country-information-report-papua-new-guinea.pdf#page=6); [Bougainville Education Act 2013](https://www.pngpie.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Bougainville-Education-Act-2013.pdf).
