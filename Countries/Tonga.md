# Unschooling in Tonga

## Compulsory enrolment and attendance

Section 98(2) of the Education Act defines compulsory school age as beginning
at 4 and continuing until the nineteenth birthday, subject to section 100.
Section 99(1) requires a parent to enrol the child at school at the beginning
of the term immediately preceding the child's fourth birthday.

Section 99(2) requires parents or guardians to secure suitable and efficient
education through regular school attendance. Its exceptions concern the
absence of a school within walking distance, alternative educational
arrangements accepted by the Minister, completion of twelve years of
education, and sickness or another unavoidable cause preventing attendance.

## Alternative educational arrangements

Under section 99(2)(b), a parent or guardian may make other arrangements for
the child's education, subject to the Minister deeming them "suitable and
efficient". This exception is not confined to children with a medical
condition or another specified personal circumstance.

The provision does not prescribe particular subjects, teaching methods or a
timetable. Section 49 separately governs school curricula: government
schools use curricula determined by the Curriculum Development Unit and
approved by the Minister; managing authorities prescribe curricula for the
schools under their control with the Minister's prior agreement.

## Exemptions under section 100

Section 100 gives the Chief Executive Officer a separate power to exempt a
parent from section 99. The grounds concern illness, injury, infection risk
or another sufficient reason preventing attendance; employment or an
apprenticeship at employable age; and suspension pending a return to school
or enrolment elsewhere.

The Chief Executive Officer determines the exemption's duration. Special
needs alone cannot justify an exemption, and section 100(4) declares the
decision final.

## Foreign families

Section 98(1) expressly covers every child living in Tonga, irrespective of
whether the child is a Tongan subject. Section 99(2)(b) imposes no nationality
condition on alternative educational arrangements.
