# Unschooling in Azerbaijan

## Compulsory education

General secondary education is compulsory in Azerbaijan. Primary education begins at age 6 and covers Grades 1–4; general secondary education covers Grades 5–9. Compulsory general education therefore extends through Grade 9.

## Home education

Azerbaijani legislation recognizes *evdə təhsil* (“home education”) as a form of education provided in the home.

Under the Law on the Education of Persons with Disabilities (Special Education), home education is defined as the acquisition of the general education curriculum at home, through teachers from the relevant educational institution, by a person whose state of health prevents attendance at an educational institution.

Eligibility is regulated by Cabinet of Ministers Decision No. 77 of 10 May 2002, which establishes a list of qualifying medical conditions and the procedure for arranging education at home. Placement is made on the basis of medical documentation and a decision of the relevant psychological-medical-pedagogical commission.

Home education remains formal education and is organized according to the applicable state education programme.

A separate *sərbəst (eksternat)* (“external”) form of education is also provided for in the education system. It applies to persons who, for specified reasons, have remained outside general education and, in certain circumstances, to pupils completing an educational level ahead of the ordinary schedule. Assessment is conducted against the curriculum applicable to formal general education.

## Statistics

Officially reported figures for *evdə təhsil* concern pupils receiving this form of home-based formal education.
