# Unschooling in Kazakhstan

## Compulsory education and attendance

Article 33(1) of the [Constitution](https://akorda.kz/public/ru/konstituciya-respubliki-kazahstan-3053339) provides (unofficial translation from Russian):

> Primary and basic secondary education are compulsory.

The [Law on Education](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z070000319_), Article 31(1), provides for admission to first grade from age six. Separately, Article 49(2)(4) requires parents and other legal representatives to (unofficial translation from Russian):

> ensure that children attend classes at the educational institution.

Article 49(3) requires parents of Kazakh children permanently resident in Kazakhstan to secure their secondary education through Kazakhstani educational institutions or licensed international schools in Kazakhstan, with an exception for children who have lawfully departed abroad. The home-based and external-study arrangements below operate within the education system.

## Individual education at home for health reasons

Article 8(5) of the [Law on Education](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z070000319_) provides individual, free education at home or in medical institutions for citizens whose health prevents prolonged attendance at a secondary-education institution.

The [official enrolment service](https://www.gov.kz/services/4301) requires a parent's application and a medical consultative commission's opinion recommending home education. The outcome is an order enrolling the child in individual free education at home; the stated processing period is two working days.

## External studies: eligibility and permission

Article 1(60) of the Law on Education defines external studies (*eksternat*) as independent study of the subjects of the relevant educational programme without regular class attendance. Paragraphs 3–4 of the [External Studies Rules, Order No. 61 of 22 January 2016](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1600013110), provide for independent or distance study. At basic and general upper-secondary levels, eligibility is restricted to:

- learners with a medical consultative commission's opinion concerning their health;
- learners who are children of Kazakh citizens temporarily resident abroad;
- learners meeting the academic condition in paragraph 4(3) (unofficial translation from Russian):

  > marks “4” and “5” in all subjects studied throughout the entire period of study.

Permission is obtained through an electronic application to the education authority, supported by the relevant medical opinion, school reports or evidence of residence/study abroad. Annex 1 of the Rules specifies a free service with a ten-working-day processing period, resulting in an extract from the permission order or a reasoned refusal. The [official electronic service](https://beta.egov.kz/services/3072) describes the application and supporting documents.

## Programme and assessment

Paragraph 8 of the [External Studies Rules](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V1600013110) requires compliance with the state compulsory education standards. Under paragraph 12, the educational institution prepares an individual study programme and consultation schedule consistent with its working curriculum for the academic year, taking account of health and assessment results. Paragraph 13 subjects external learners to the national rules on assessment under Order No. 125 of 18 March 2008, including intermediate and final certification.

## Official statistics

In its [2026 report on state policy concerning children](https://www.gov.kz/org/edu/press/news/details/1170676?lang=ru), the Ministry of Education reported that approximately **17,000 children were educated at home**, among more than 237,000 receiving psychological, medical and pedagogical support. This figure concerns home education within that support provision.
