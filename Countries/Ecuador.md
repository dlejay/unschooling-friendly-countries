# Unschooling in Ecuador

## Compulsory education

Education is compulsory from the level of Educación Inicial through Bachillerato.

The ordinary educational sequence comprises approximately 15 years:

- 2 years of Educación Inicial, ordinarily at ages 3–4;
- 10 years of Educación General Básica, ordinarily at ages 5–14; and
- 3 years of Bachillerato, ordinarily at ages 15–17.

Children below the age of 3 remain principally the responsibility of the family.

## Home education

Home education is expressly recognised as a form of formal distance education (*educación a distancia, tipo en casa*). It is available to children of ordinary school age from Educación Inicial through Bachillerato General, option in Sciences.

Under *Acuerdo MINEDEC-MINEDEC-2026-00023-A*, a homeschooled student must be enrolled in an educational institution authorised to provide this form of education.

A parent or legal representative may act as the child's educational tutor only if they hold a third-level qualification in education or a related field. Otherwise, the family must provide one or more tutors holding such a qualification throughout the school year.

The tutor must prepare an individual study plan and pedagogical proposal. These must comply with the national curriculum, ensure attainment of its learning objectives, and be approved by the educational institution.

Diagnostic and formative assessment is undertaken by the parent or tutor and documented in a portfolio submitted to the institution. Summative assessments are compulsory and are administered by the institution, either in person or under supervised online conditions.

The framework therefore permits substantial individualisation of home education, but not autonomous interest-driven unschooling outside the national curriculum.

The current rules were introduced by *Acuerdo MINEDEC-MINEDEC-2026-00023-A*, issued on 6 April 2026 and published in the *Registro Oficial* on 20 April 2026. It repealed *Acuerdo MINEDUC-MINEDUC-2025-00024-A*, which had itself repealed the 2023 home-education rules.
