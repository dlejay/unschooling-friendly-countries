# Unschooling in Rwanda

Education is governed by [Law No. 010/2021 of 16 February 2021 determining
the organisation of education](https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/LAWS/01_LAW_DETERMINING_THE_ORGANIZATION_OF_EDUCATION.pdf).
Article 137 repealed Law No. 36/2018 of 29 June 2018 governing education.
The quotations below reproduce the English text published in the Official
Gazette.

## Compulsory education

[Article 55](https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/LAWS/01_LAW_DETERMINING_THE_ORGANIZATION_OF_EDUCATION.pdf#page=68)
provides:

> Primary education is organised in a single cycle of six (6) years.

[Article 56](https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/LAWS/01_LAW_DETERMINING_THE_ORGANIZATION_OF_EDUCATION.pdf#page=69)
specifies the age of entry:

> A child starts primary school at the age of at least six (6) years old.

Article 57 provides:

> Primary education is compulsory and free both in public and
> Government-subsidised schools. In these schools, the cost of education
> is covered by the government.
>
> A parent or any other person with parental authority over the child is
> obliged to cause admission of the child to school if the child attains
> the age of commencing primary education.
>
> No child may be deprived of the right to attend primary school. A
> parent or any other person with parental authority over the child
> prevents the child from any reason that may cause school dropout.

## Education within the family

[Article 122](https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/LAWS/01_LAW_DETERMINING_THE_ORGANIZATION_OF_EDUCATION.pdf#page=113)
provides:

> Education within the family is provided by parents, guardian and
> people who stay with the child. Its aim is to provide the child with
> knowledge and ethical values.

## School attendance and enforcement

[Article 125](https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/LAWS/01_LAW_DETERMINING_THE_ORGANIZATION_OF_EDUCATION.pdf#page=115)
provides, in part:

> Subject to the provisions of other penal laws, a parent or any other
> person with parental authority over a child:
>
> 1º who refuses a child the right to start primary school while he or
> she has attained the age of commencement;
>
> 2º Who makes a child not to attend class for no sound reason;
>
> [...]
>
> 5º Who contributes to the child’s school dropout; commits a fault.

The same article specifies the sanction:

> He or she is liable to an administrative fine of at least five thousand
> Rwandan francs (FRW 5,000) but not exceeding ten thousand (FRW 10,000)
> and the child is reinstated in his or her rights.
