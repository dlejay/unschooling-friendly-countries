# Unschooling in North Macedonia

## Compulsory schooling

Article 8 of the Law on Primary Education makes primary education compulsory for nine years. Under article 60(6), parents must enrol children who turn six by the end of the calendar year; articles 60(7)–(8) provide limited exceptions for earlier admission or postponement. Article 63 establishes the parental duty to enrol the child in school. [Law on Primary Education, articles 8, 60 and 63](https://portal.mdt.gov.mk/post-body-files/zakoni-mon-file-AeUI.docx).

Article 3 of the Law on Secondary Education makes secondary education compulsory for every citizen. Its article 2 provides for general and vocational secondary programmes. The obligation is therefore not limited to the nine years of primary education. [Law on Secondary Education, articles 2–3](https://portal.mdt.gov.mk/post-body-files/zakoni-mon-file-LCqK.docx).

## Home education

### Eligibility and organisation

Article 15(3) of the Law on Primary Education states:

> Училиштето организира воспитно-образовен процес во домашни услови како посебен облик на работа за ученици на подолго домашно лекување.

This provision concerns school-organised education for pupils undergoing prolonged treatment at home. Article 15(4) specifies serious health problems or chronic illness preventing attendance for more than three weeks. Parents notify the school; under article 15(6), the school where the pupil is enrolled delivers instruction remotely, in person or through a combination. This health-based provision does not establish a general parental option to home educate. [Article 15(3)–(6)](https://portal.mdt.gov.mk/post-body-files/zakoni-mon-file-AeUI.docx).

At secondary level, article 4-a(3)–(5) likewise provides for school-organised instruction during prolonged treatment. Where serious health problems or chronic illness prevent attendance for more than six weeks, the school may organise home or hospital instruction remotely, in person or through a combination. Parents notify the school of the need. [Law on Secondary Education, article 4-a](https://portal.mdt.gov.mk/post-body-files/zakoni-mon-file-LCqK.docx).

### Other secondary pathways

Article 41 provides an external-student route (*вонредни ученици*) based on examinations under a school curriculum and examination programme. Eligibility includes being older than 17, sick leave exceeding six months, employment and certain other specified circumstances or cases established by the school's statute. This remains a school-based qualification route with prescribed programmes. [Article 41(3)–(6)](https://portal.mdt.gov.mk/post-body-files/zakoni-mon-file-LCqK.docx).

### Recent implementation

On 6 August 2025, the Ministry announced the introduction of formal education in healthcare institutions from September 2025 for pupils hospitalised for more than three weeks, in cooperation with the Ministry of Health and the association SANO. The measure supports educational continuity during treatment. [Ministry announcement, 6 August 2025](https://mon.gov.mk/mk-MK/odnosi-so-javnost/novosti/se-voveduva-formalno-obrazovanie-vo-zdravstveni-ustanovi-ucenicite-na-podolgo-bolnicko-lekuvanje-povekje-nema-da-gubat-nastava).

## Statistics

The State Statistical Office's release of 7 April 2026 gives the following figures for the **beginning of school year 2025–2026**:

| Regular school sector | Schools | Pupils | Change in pupils from previous school year |
| --------------------- | ------: | -----: | ----------------------------------------: |
| Primary and lower secondary | 927 | 174,040 | −3.6% |
| Upper secondary | 128 | 67,540 | +0.6% |

These figures cover regular schools; they do not measure elective home education or separately identify pupils receiving school-organised instruction at home. [State Statistical Office, school year 2025–2026](https://www.stat.mk/en/stat/population-and-living-conditions/education/primary-lower-secondary-and-upper-secondary-schools-at-the-beginning-of-the-school-year/primary-lower-secondary-and-upper-secondary-schools-at-the-beginning-of-the-school-year-20252026/).
