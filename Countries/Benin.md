# Unschooling in Benin

## Compulsory schooling

Article 113 of the Child Code, Law No. 2015-08 of 8 December 2015, makes schooling compulsory from preschool to the end of primary education. It expressly requires parents to enrol their children in school. Article 114 provides for free preschool and primary education in public schools. [Child Code, Articles 113–114, p.28](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2015-08/download#page=28).

The Education Framework Law provides for two years of preschool and a normal primary cycle of six years, from *Cours d’Initiation* (CI) to *Cours Moyen deuxième année* (CM2). Primary education ends with the *Certificat d’Études Primaires* (CEP) examination. [Law No. 2003-17 of 11 November 2003, rectified by Law No. 2005-33 of 6 October 2005, Articles 23–26, pp.4–5](https://www.unesco.org/education/edurights/media/docs/a244de65e69a2204a88fd68a9c43694f2ed04564.pdf#page=4).

## Home education

The enrolment obligation in Article 113 of the Child Code is explicit. In translation :

> Parents are required to enrol their children in school without any form of discrimination.

Article 116 also restricts school withdrawal before obtaining the CEP or reaching CM2, with an exception for a child with an intellectual or other disability. That exception is tied to the child's circumstances and does not establish a general parental-choice exemption. [Child Code, Articles 113 and 116, p.28](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2015-08/download#page=28).

Article 15 of the Education Framework Law places teaching at the different educational levels in public and private institutions opened with ministerial authorisation. Read together with the Child Code's enrolment requirement, these provisions do not give parents a general right to replace school enrolment with autonomous home education. [Education Framework Law, Article 15, p.3](https://www.unesco.org/education/edurights/media/docs/a244de65e69a2204a88fd68a9c43694f2ed04564.pdf#page=3).

## Statistics

The national CEP examination provides the following figures for the 2026 session :

| Year | Registered candidates | Candidates who sat | Candidates admitted | Pass rate |
| ---- | --------------------: | -----------------: | ------------------: | --------: |
| 2026 | 286,995 | 281,405 | 253,742 | 90.17% |

The Government reported the registration total at the launch of the examination on 1 June 2026. Public broadcaster SRTB reported the attendance, admissions and pass rate announced by the Directorate of Examinations and Competitions on 26 June. These figures concern the national primary-school leaving examination cohort, not the number of home-educated children. [Government — CEP 2026 launch, 1 June 2026](https://www.gouv.bj/article/3526/cep-2026-ministre-armand-kuyema-natta-donne-coup-envoi-epreuves-aplahoue/) ; [SRTB — CEP results, 26 June 2026](https://srtb.bj/cep-2026-plus-de-90-des-candidats-declares-admis/).
