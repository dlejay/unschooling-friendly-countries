# Unschooling in Kosovo

## Compulsory education

Article 9 of Law No. 04/L-032 on Pre-University Education provides for five
years of primary education and four years of lower secondary education.
Under article 9(2), compulsory schooling begins at the start of the school
year following the child's sixth birthday and continues until completion
of lower secondary education, ISCED level 2.

Articles 18 and 37 of Law No. 08/L-153 on Early Childhood Education add
compulsory pre-primary education from age 5, with effect from the 2024–2025
school year. Pre-primary education is organised within primary schools and
preschool institutions.

## Home education and authorisation

Article 15(2)(2.1) of Law No. 04/L-032 requires parents to enrol a child of
compulsory school age in an appropriate educational institution or to make
alternative educational arrangements approved by the Ministry under article
47. Failure to meet that requirement is an offence.

Article 47, entitled "Home schooling", addresses education outside the formal
public or private education and training system. Under paragraph 4, both the
parents' educational arrangements and the inspection arrangements require
Ministry approval following consultation with the relevant municipality.

## Educational standard

Article 47(2) requires efficient, full-time education suited to the child's
age, ability, aptitude and any special educational needs. The article does
not prescribe a subject list or a fixed teaching timetable.

Article 24 separately establishes the Kosovo Curriculum Framework, comprising
a core curriculum and a school-based curriculum. Paragraph 7 requires public
and private educational institutions to follow the core curriculum. It permits
departures in exceptional circumstances with Ministry permission, for
approved innovative projects, or under an approved individual education plan.

## Municipal supervision

Under article 47(1), where a municipality considers that a child of compulsory
school age in its territory is not receiving suitable education, whether at
school or otherwise, it must send the parents a written notice. The notice
sets a deadline for the parents to satisfy the municipality that suitable
education is being provided at home or elsewhere. This provision excludes
children subject to permanent expulsion under the Law.

If the parents fail to respond within the specified period, article 47(3)
requires the municipality to proceed with the case as a minor offence.

## Early childhood education in the family

Article 4(1)(1.2) of Law No. 08/L-153 defines early childhood education in the
family as education, care and development provided by parents or legal
guardians, with or without institutional support. Article 17 provides for
support from birth until entry into the first grade of primary school,
including for children unable to attend preschool because of illness.
The forms of support are to be regulated by a subordinate legal act.

## Foreign families

Article 47 of Law No. 04/L-032 concerns children within the municipality's
territory and imposes no citizenship condition on home education. Article
6(3) of Law No. 08/L-153 likewise recognises the right of all children within
Kosovo's territory to quality early childhood education.
