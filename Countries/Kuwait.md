# Unschooling in Kuwait

All quotations below are unofficial translations from Arabic.

## Compulsory education and school attendance

Article 1 of [Law No. 11 of 1965 on Compulsory Education, as amended by
Law No. 25 of 2014](https://mesferlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screen-Shot-2023-06-02-at-6.21.27-PM-1-1.pdf#page=1),
provides, in part:

> Education shall be compulsory and free for all Kuwaiti children, male
> and female, from the beginning of the primary stage through the
> intermediate stage [...].

[Article 2](https://mesferlaw.com/قانون-رقم-11-لسنة-1965-في-شان-التعليم-الالزام/)
provides, in part:

> Compulsory education for a child begins at the age of six according to
> the Gregorian calendar and remains compulsory throughout the period
> prescribed by the administrative regulations and rules [...].

Article 12 specifies that compulsory education extends to the end of the
intermediate stage. The Ministry of Education's [Comprehensive Regulations
for Education, 2026, p. 162](https://newmoesitev2.blob.core.windows.net/files/uploads/41cf784f-5c24-4fb3-a59c-107cd0e25c11.pdf#page=162),
place grades 1–5 in the primary stage and grades 6–9 in the intermediate
stage.

[Article 9 of Law No. 11 of 1965, as amended in 2014](https://mesferlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screen-Shot-2023-06-02-at-6.21.27-PM-1-1.pdf#page=2),
provides, in part:

> If the guardian does not enrol the child in school at the prescribed
> time, or the child does not attend regularly without an acceptable
> excuse, the school principal must notify the guardian by registered
> letter with acknowledgement of receipt, or by any means at the
> guardian's known place of residence.
>
> If the child is not enrolled within one week of receipt of the
> registered letter, or is again absent from school without reasonable
> cause, the guardian shall be considered in breach of this Law. The
> school principal must notify the Ministry of Education of the matter
> within no more than two weeks [...].

Article 10, first paragraph, provides:

> A child's guardian who contravenes the preceding article shall be
> punished by imprisonment for a term not exceeding one month and a fine
> not exceeding 1,000 dinars, or either of these penalties.

## Nationality and the scope of compulsory education

The general rules accompanying the age table in [Part VIII, Chapter II,
article 1 of the 2026 Regulations, p. 162](https://newmoesitev2.blob.core.windows.net/files/uploads/41cf784f-5c24-4fb3-a59c-107cd0e25c11.pdf#page=162),
state:

> Law No. 11 of 1965 on Compulsory Education, as amended by Law No. 25
> of 2014, applies with respect to the compulsory age in the primary and
> intermediate stages to Kuwaitis only.

## Private educational institutions and study abroad

[Article 6 of Law No. 11 of 1965, as amended in 2014](https://mesferlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screen-Shot-2023-06-02-at-6.21.27-PM-1-1.pdf#page=1),
provides:

> The person upon whom the obligation falls may have the child educated
> at a private educational institution, provided that study at that
> institution is equivalent to compulsory education. All public and
> private institutions must notify the Ministry of Education of the names
> of children subject to this Law who are registered with them at least
> one week before the beginning of the school year. They must also notify
> the Ministry of Education of the names of all children subject to the
> obligation who leave school. The duty to notify falls upon the guardian
> if the child studies outside the country.

Article 7 provides:

> The private educational institutions whose instruction is considered
> equivalent to the instruction prescribed for compulsory education shall
> be specified by ministerial decision.

## Medical exemptions

[Article 4 of Law No. 11 of 1965, as amended in 2014](https://mesferlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Screen-Shot-2023-06-02-at-6.21.27-PM-1-1.pdf#page=1),
provides:

> A child shall be exempt from compulsory education if the child has an
> illness preventing continued study, or a physical or mental disability
> preventing regular attendance at special-education schools or equivalent
> educational institutions established by the Ministry of Education for
> persons with special needs, provided that the guardian submits evidence
> of this.

[Article 5](https://mesferlaw.com/قانون-رقم-11-لسنة-1965-في-شان-التعليم-الالزام/)
provides:

> In all cases, exemption shall be granted by a decision of the Minister
> of Education. The exemption shall remain in force for as long as its
> grounds persist; if those grounds cease to exist, the obligation shall
> resume automatically.

## The home-study system

The Ministry of Education's [2026–2027 calendar](https://www.moe.edu.kw/pages/calendar)
provides a registration period from 20 September 2026 to 31 January 2027 for:

> Registration of applicants for the home-study system in the secondary
> grades, for general education and private education.

Part XVII, Chapter II, article 9 of the [2026 Regulations, pp. 257–258](https://newmoesitev2.blob.core.windows.net/files/uploads/41cf784f-5c24-4fb3-a59c-107cd0e25c11.pdf#page=257),
governs this system, termed *niẓām al-manāzil* (نظام المنازل). Its
registration conditions include:

> The student must not be enrolled in any public or private school or
> evening centre, and must sign a declaration to that effect, or must
> submit a school-leaving certificate for the school year in which the
> student registers before the first study-period examinations begin.

Article 9 permits registration by Kuwaitis and persons treated as such,
Gulf Cooperation Council citizens, residents in an irregular situation
holding a valid Central Agency card, and other nationals holding valid
residence permits. Required documents include an authenticated certificate
of passing the last completed grade, identification documents, a copy of
the birth certificate and recent photographs. Registration follows the
educational calendar, and fees are determined by the competent authorities.
