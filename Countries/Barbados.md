# Unschooling in Barbados

## Compulsory education

Under the Education Act, compulsory school age runs from age 5 to age 16.

Parents must ensure that children of compulsory school age receive full-time education suitable to their age and ability, either through regular attendance at a public or private school or through another arrangement satisfactory to the Minister.

## Home education

Section 42 of the Education Act allows a child to be exempted from compulsory school attendance when the child is receiving instruction at home in a manner and to a standard satisfactory to the Minister.

This is a prior-permission system rather than a notification system. A parent must apply to the Chief Education Officer for a certificate of exemption. The certificate may be granted or refused, although a refusal may be appealed to the Minister.

The Ministry’s published process requires:

* a letter requesting permission and explaining the reason for home education;
* a completed application form;
* the proposed curriculum;
* a timetable;
* the names and qualifications of the instructors or tutors; and
* an interview with the education authorities.

The application form requires the timetable to specify subjects and teaching times. It must include English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies and physical activity. Certified copies of the instructors’ qualifications must also be supplied.

Permission must be renewed annually. School attendance officers may visit the home to verify that the approved home-education programme is being conducted.

These requirements do not appear compatible with fully autonomous, curriculum-free or timetable-free unschooling. A child’s interests could potentially influence activities and teaching methods, but only within a structured programme accepted by the Ministry.

## Foreign families

The official application form accommodates both resident and non-resident applicants, and the Ministry also publishes the home-education process for Welcome Stamp families. Foreign and non-resident families must therefore obtain the same prior permission; their immigration status does not provide an automatic exemption from the education requirements.
