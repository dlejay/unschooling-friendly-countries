# Unschooling in Palestine

The quotations below are unofficial English translations of the Arabic text.

## Compulsory schooling

[Article 5(1) of Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 on General Education](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/21632)
provides:

> Education shall be compulsory until the end of grade ten.

Article 9(2) requires institutional enrolment:

> Every parent or guardian must enrol the children under their parental
> authority or guardianship in basic education institutions.

Article 10(1) sets the admission age:

> A pupil shall be admitted to the first grade of basic education if they
> have reached five years and seven months of age by the end of August of
> the year in which they will enter school.

## Preschool education

[Article 7(4)](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/21632)
provides:

> The Ministry shall work to expand its services at the kindergarten stage
> towards free and compulsory education at this stage, in accordance with
> a unified curriculum framework approved by the Ministry.

## Penalties

[Article 50](https://mjr.ogb.gov.ps/Decrees/ViewText/21632)
prescribes up to one year's imprisonment, a fine of up to JOD 1,000 or its
equivalent in circulating currency, or both, for:

> 1. Any parent who refuses to enrol, or withdraws, their son or daughter
> from an educational institution before the age of sixteen.

## Education during emergencies

Article 51 requires the Ministry to provide education during emergencies.

In its [announcement of 19 September 2026](https://moe.pna.ps/c/30795),
the Ministry described provision for the 2026/2027 school year for pupils
from Gaza. Alongside in-person schooling, it operates virtual schools for
pupils outside Gaza and for pupils within Gaza who cannot reach in-person
schools.
