# Unschooling in the Marshall Islands

## Compulsory enrolment and attendance

Section 318(1) of the [Marshall Islands Public School System Act 2013](https://rmiparliament.org/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2013/2013-0023/2013-0023_1.pdf#page=16) sets entry at age five, reached by the beginning of the school year, and provides:

> The enrolment and attendance of a child under this Subsection shall be compulsory

Section 320(1) places responsibility on parents and guardians for enrolment and attendance from ages **5 to 18**, upon eligibility for elementary or secondary school. Section 319 permits attendance at the local public school or a non-public school.

## Exceptions to attendance

Regulation 14-10-3(a) of the [Ministry of Education Rules and Regulations, printed p. 20](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ToAb4_fcuw0vwYcmOhsr3IbaAbRWfQ9X/view) requires attendance unless the child is:

> excluded or excepted from attendance by the Commissioner for compelling or legitimate reasons

The exception depends on the Commissioner's decision and the reasons justifying it. Regulation 14-10-4(c) provides for court enforcement of mandatory attendance.

## Non-public schools

Under sections 331–332 of the [Act, pp. 19–20](https://rmiparliament.org/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2013/2013-0023/2013-0023_1.pdf#page=19), establishing a non-public school requires a prior written charter application to the Commissioner, specifying its location, instruction, language, teacher qualifications and enrolment. The National Board of Education approves the charter. Attendance at an unchartered school does not satisfy the statutory attendance requirement.

## Curriculum and assessment

Section 315 requires national minimum instructional and curriculum standards, including compulsory Marshallese language, custom, culture and history in schools. Section 321 provides for assessment and reporting of student progress and promotion based on academic performance.

For non-public schools, [regulations 14-27-7 and 14-27-8, printed pp. 48–49](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ToAb4_fcuw0vwYcmOhsr3IbaAbRWfQ9X/view), prescribe a sequential programme covering English and Marshallese language arts, mathematics, social studies, science, art, music, health/population and physical education. Instruction must meet the Board's minimum standards and equal public-school instructional time; professional personnel require ministry certification.

## Official statistics

The [Public School System Annual Report for school year 2025–2026, section 3.2, p. 16](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jlPgDai6hHq1SFXsJunCzKhM5h5t1eGG/view), submitted to the Nitijela in August 2026, records **11,632 pupils**: **609 preschool**, **7,883 primary** and **3,140 secondary**. Public schools account for **8,913** pupils and private schools for **2,719**. These are school-enrolment figures.
