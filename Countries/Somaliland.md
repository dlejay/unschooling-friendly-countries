# Unschooling in Somaliland

## Compulsory education

Education is not legally compulsory in Somaliland.

Article 15 of the Somaliland Constitution states that primary education should be free and given priority, but does not make education or school attendance compulsory.

The National Education Law (Law No. 77/2018) similarly does not establish a general duty for parents to enrol their children in school.

Consequently, parents do not need a homeschooling exemption or permission to keep their children outside the school system.

## Statistics

In 2020, the Somaliland Government, Education Cannot Wait and UNICEF reported that more than 51 per cent of children in Somaliland were out of school.
