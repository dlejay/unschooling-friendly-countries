# Home education in the Comoros

## Compulsory schooling

Law No.20-034/AU of 29 December 2020, promulgated by Decree No.21-005/PR of 30 January 2021, revises the education framework. Article 4 makes schooling compulsory from **3 to 16 years** and expressly requires school enrolment and continued schooling:

> […] an obligation for parents to enrol their children in school and keep them there at least until the end of compulsory schooling.

Unofficial English translation of an excerpt from [article 4, p.2; original French text](https://justice.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/decret-n-21-005pr-portant-promulgation-de-la-loi-n-20-034au-du-29-decembre-2020-portant-revision-de-la-loi-n-94-035af-relative-a-lorientation-et-a-leducation.pdf).

## Parental and family education

Article 2 describes parental education as supporting children's social integration and their acquisition of the benefits of the education system throughout their school and university education. Article 7 characterises family and social education as complementing pre-primary education. [Articles 2 and 7, pp.2–3](https://justice.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/decret-n-21-005pr-portant-promulgation-de-la-loi-n-20-034au-du-29-decembre-2020-portant-revision-de-la-loi-n-94-035af-relative-a-lorientation-et-a-leducation.pdf).

## School curriculum and assessment

Article 4 requires a common foundation of knowledge and competencies by age 16. Private educational establishments must follow official ministry programmes under article 35; article 38 requires their pupils to be assessed on the same basis and under the same conditions as public-school pupils. [Articles 4, 35 and 38, pp.2 and 8](https://justice.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/decret-n-21-005pr-portant-promulgation-de-la-loi-n-20-034au-du-29-decembre-2020-portant-revision-de-la-loi-n-94-035af-relative-a-lorientation-et-a-leducation.pdf).
