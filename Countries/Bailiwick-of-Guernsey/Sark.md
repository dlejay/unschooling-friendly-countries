# Unschooling in Sark

## Compulsory education

Children living in Sark must receive full-time education between the ages of 5 and 16.

## Home education

According to the Education (Sark) Ordinance, 2003:

> **Duty of parents to secure the education of their children**
>
> **3. (1)** It shall be the duty of the parent of every child of compulsory
> school age to cause him to receive efficient full-time education suitable to his age,
> ability and aptitude and to any special educational needs which he may have, either
> by regular attendance at school or otherwise.
>
> **(2)** Where a parent elects to cause a child to receive education
> otherwise than by regular attendance at school in Sark or elsewhere, the Committee
> may arrange for assessment of the education so provided to be undertaken by a
> representative of the Committee, or by another person appointed by the Committee
> for that purpose, to ascertain whether, in the opinion of the Committee, the child is
> receiving efficient full-time education suitable to his age, ability and aptitude and to
> any special educational needs which he may have.

Home education does not require prior permission. However, families moving to Sark,
or already living there and educating a child at home, are asked to register each child
with the Education Committee.

The Education Committee's mandate also provides for an annual Home Education Plan,
agreed between the parents or carers and the Committee, and for the Committee to
monitor its implementation.

The legislation does not prescribe a curriculum or require education to reproduce
school methods. An interest-driven approach is therefore possible, provided the
overall education is efficient, full-time and suitable to the child's age, ability,
aptitude and any special educational needs. Fully autonomous unschooling may
nevertheless be subject to scrutiny through the annual plan and the Committee's
assessment powers.
