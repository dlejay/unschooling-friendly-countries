# Unschooling in Jordan

## Compulsory education

The Education and Human Resources Development Law No. 13 of 2026 was
published on 14 May 2026 and entered into force 90 days later. It repealed
the Education Law No. 3 of 1994, on which the previous version of this page
was based.

Article 11 makes education compulsory from grade 1 through grade 10 and
free in government educational institutions. A child may be admitted to
grade 1 if they turn 6 by 31 December of the school year in which they are
admitted.

## Home education

Jordan has a Ministry-run **Non-Regular Learners (Home Studies) Programme**,
but it is not a general right to homeschool or unschool.

The Ministry's current service guide requires an applicant to:

- be at least 12 years old;
- have been out of education for at least one academic year; and
- provide educational records or take a placement test if records are not
  available.

Admission requires an application to the education directorate and an
official approval. Participants study the prescribed curriculum on their
own and sit the regular end-of-semester examinations at a government
school. The ordinary rules on passing, completion and failure apply.

The 2026 Law authorises the Minister to regulate admission to formal and
non-formal programmes. Article 28 keeps the regulations, instructions and
decisions made under the repealed education law in force until they are
repealed, amended or replaced.
