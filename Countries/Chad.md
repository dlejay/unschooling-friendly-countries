# Unschooling in Chad

## Compulsory schooling

Article 21 of Law No. 016/PR/2006 of 13 March makes fundamental education compulsory. Article 4 guarantees this education to young people aged six to sixteen. It comprises six years of primary education and four years of lower-secondary education (*enseignement moyen*). Articles 25 and 29 provide for delivery in primary schools and general-education colleges respectively. [Education Framework Law, Articles 4, 21 and 23–29, printed pp.1 and 6–7](https://mesrsfp.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Loi-N%C2%B016-PR-2006-Portant-Orientation-du-Systeme-Educatif-Tchadien.pdf).

The obligation concerns school enrolment, rather than only the provision of instruction. Article 369 of the Penal Code, enacted by Law No. 001/PR/2017 of 8 May, provides, in translation :

> A parent who refuses to have their child enrolled in school is liable to a fine of CFA francs 50,000 to 500,000.

The offence expressly addresses refusal to enrol a child at school ; it is not limited to failure to provide any education. [Penal Code, Article 369, printed p.91](https://sherloc.unodc.org/cld/uploads/res/document/code-pnal_html/Tchad-Code-penal-2017.pdf#page=91).

## Home education and other forms of learning

The Education Framework Law recognises several forms of learning outside ordinary classrooms. Article 8 recognises distance education as an alternative mode of education and training, with its organisation, operation and supervision governed by separate texts. Articles 64–67 provide for non-formal education outside public and private schools, including literacy and specialised training centres, with educational content subject to technical supervision. [Education Framework Law, Articles 8 and 64–67, printed pp.2 and 14](https://mesrsfp.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Loi-N%C2%B016-PR-2006-Portant-Orientation-du-Systeme-Educatif-Tchadien.pdf).

Articles 68–69 recognise informal education through everyday experience and interaction with one's environment, including the family, community and sociocultural groups. This recognition does not establish an exemption from the school-enrolment obligation or a general parental right to replace compulsory schooling with independent home education. Distance education and informal family learning should therefore be distinguished from a general home-education option. [Education Framework Law, Articles 68–71, printed pp.14–15](https://mesrsfp.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Loi-N%C2%B016-PR-2006-Portant-Orientation-du-Systeme-Educatif-Tchadien.pdf) ; [Penal Code, Article 369](https://sherloc.unodc.org/cld/uploads/res/document/code-pnal_html/Tchad-Code-penal-2017.pdf#page=91).

## Recent developments

**2026.** UNESCO's International Bureau of Education reported on 17 October that an N'Djamena workshop had finalised the revised CM1 and CM2 programmes under the *Programme de Refondation de l'École Tchadienne* (PRET), completing the rewriting of programmes across the primary cycle. The work addressed competencies, teaching methods and assessment, and defined directions for implementation and monitoring of experimentation. Finalisation of these programmes should be distinguished from nationwide classroom implementation. [UNESCO-IBE — primary curriculum reform, 17 October 2026](https://www.ibe.unesco.org/fr/articles/tchad-une-etape-decisive-vers-la-validation-des-programmes-scolaires-de-cm1-et-cm2-dans-le-cadre-du).

## Statistics

The Ministry's *Annuaire statistique de l'éducation 2024–2025*, published through INSEED, reports the following national primary-school enrolments :

| School year | Primary-school pupils |
| ----------- | --------------------: |
| 2024–2025 | 3,206,095 |
| 2023–2024 | 3,049,526 |

The 2024–2025 total includes 1,470,917 girls, representing 45.9% of pupils. The figures cover public, community and private schools ; they are not counts of home-educated or unschooling children. [MENPC — Education Statistical Yearbook 2024–2025, Box 02, printed p.35](https://www.inseed.td/inseed/index.php/component/jdownloads/send/43-annuaires-statistiques-de-l-education/548-annuaire-statistique-de-l-education-2024-2025).
