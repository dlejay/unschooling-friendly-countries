# Unschooling in the Arab Republic of Egypt

## Compulsory schooling

Article 19 of the Constitution makes education compulsory until the end of secondary education or its equivalent. Article 4 of the Education Law, as substituted by Law No. 169 of 2025, specifies twelve years of compulsory education : six primary years, three preparatory years and three secondary years. [Constitution, Article 19](https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/The_Egyptian_constitution_e071492e20.pdf) ; [Law No. 169 of 2025, Article 1 substituting Education Law Article 4, p.11](https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/105925/OG32_20250813_5008.pdf#page=11).

Article 15 of Education Law No. 139 of 1981, as amended, obliges parents or guardians to provide their Egyptian children's nine-year basic education from age six. Governors allocate children to schools. Admission at five and a half is possible where places are available. [Education Law, Article 15, p.5](https://moe.gov.eg/media/l3hnjmv4/educationactno139of1981.pdf#page=5).

Article 19 enforces school enrolment and attendance. A child’s non-enrolment or ten days of unjustified absence, consecutive or intermittent, requires a warning to the parent or guardian. Failure to attend within a week of receiving it, or renewed unjustified absence, triggers the penalties in Article 21. [Education Law, Articles 19–21, pp.6–7](https://moe.gov.eg/media/l3hnjmv4/educationactno139of1981.pdf#page=6).

## Home education and external examinations

Read together, the basic-education enrolment and attendance duties preclude replacing school with autonomous home education solely by parental choice. An acceptable excuse for absence is not a general exemption. [Education Law, Articles 15 and 19–21](https://moe.gov.eg/media/l3hnjmv4/educationactno139of1981.pdf#page=5).

External examination arrangements nevertheless exist. Article 28, as substituted in 2025, allows candidates to take the general secondary leaving examination externally, subject to ministerial conditions. The Minister determines the examined subjects, curricula and entry requirements. This is a regulated route to a school qualification. [Law No. 169 of 2025, Article 1 substituting Education Law Article 28, p.13](https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/105925/OG32_20250813_5008.pdf#page=13).

The *manazel* (external-candidate) status operates within the examination system. Guidance published by Cairo Governorate on 18 October 2026 describes an 85% attendance requirement for regular secondary students and possible transfer to *manazel* status when that requirement is not met. This secondary examination status does not confer a general right to withdraw a child from compulsory basic schooling or dispense with prescribed curricula and examinations. [Cairo Governorate — Ministry guidance on secondary examination entry, 18 October 2026](https://cairo.gov.eg/ar/news/2026/october/18/c7da9d16554247a2b7780e3c9dcdabaa) ; [Education Law, Articles 15 and 19](https://moe.gov.eg/media/l3hnjmv4/educationactno139of1981.pdf#page=5) ; [amended Article 28](https://mediadr.sis.gov.eg/bitstream/handle/123456789/105925/OG32_20250813_5008.pdf#page=13).

## Recent developments

**July 2026.** The Ministry of Education and Technical Education stated that “home schooling” was contrary to law and that no international school had been licensed to operate that system. It warned schools against implementing or promoting it and required licensed schools to follow approved curricula. Cairo Governorate reproduced the statement on 13 July. This warning concerns practices marketed as “home schooling” by international schools ; it should be distinguished from the regulated external-candidate arrangements above. [Cairo Governorate — Ministry statement, 13 July 2026](https://www.cairo.gov.eg/ar/news/2026/july/13/261287da83424745ae201ac3124d5c7b).

## Statistics

CAPMAS's World Teachers' Day release of 5 October 2026, as reported by *Amwal Al Ghad*, gave the following national enrolment figure :

| School year | Pupils in pre-university education |
| ----------- | --------------------------------: |
| 2025–2026 | Approximately 29.1 million |

The total covers general, technical and Al-Azhar pre-university education. It is a school-system enrolment figure, not a count of home-educated children or *manazel* candidates. [Amwal Al Ghad — reporting CAPMAS, 5 October 2026](https://amwalalghad.com/h9m3).
