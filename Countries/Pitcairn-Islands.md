# Unschooling in Pitcairn, Henderson, Ducie and Oeno Islands

## Compulsory education

[Section 23(a) of the Summary Offences Ordinance](https://www.government.pn/s/Cap-5-Summary-Offences.pdf#page=7)
applies to the parent or guardian of a child residing on Pitcairn Island
who has reached the age of five but has not yet reached sixteen.
Under section 23(b), that parent or guardian commits an offence if he or she:

> without just cause, refuses or neglects to keep that child in regular
> attendance at the public school on Pitcairn Island

The maximum fine is $4,000. [Section 2(2) of Ordinance No. 2 of 2023](https://www.government.pn/s/2023-05-31-Published-Summary-Offences-Penalties-Amendment-Ordinance.pdf#page=2)
increased the previous $200 maximum to $4,000. The amendment was published
on 31 May 2023 and entered into force the following day under section 1(2).

## Constitutional right to private schooling

[Section 17(1) of the Constitution](https://www.government.pn/s/The-Constitution-of-Pitcairn.pdf#page=9)
establishes a right to free primary education for children of the age
prescribed by law, subject to subsection (2). Section 17(2) entitles
parents and legal guardians to have their children:

> educated, at his or her own expense unless a law otherwise provides,
> in a private school

The provision defines a private school as one not established by the
Government of Pitcairn or a public authority. It also protects parents'
choice of religious and moral education in such a school.

Section 17(3) permits legislation necessary in a democratic society to
make private schools' operation conditional on minimum educational
standards, including staff qualifications, and standards concerning public
order, morality or health. These conditions must be no more onerous than
those applicable to government or public-authority schools.

## Public-school curriculum and attendance monitoring

The [Government's Education Policy, reviewed on 11 November 2021](https://www.government.pn/s/GPI-Policy-Education.pdf#page=2),
provides for Pulau School's curriculum to be developed by the Education
Officer and approved by the Trustees. It is based on the New Zealand
curriculum and incorporates Pitkern language, culture and community life
skills.

[Administration Guideline 6](https://www.government.pn/s/GPI-Policy-Education.pdf#page=5)
states:

> The Education Officer reports truancy to the Trustees

The Trustees investigate the matter in association with the Pitcairn Island
Police and the Government of Pitcairn.
