# Unschooling in the Maldives

The statutory quotations below reproduce the Ministry of Education's
functional English translation of the Education Act.

## Compulsory schooling

[Section 14 of the Education Act (No. 24/2020)](https://www.moe.gov.mv/storage/files/resources/English_translation_of_Education_Act.pdf#page=11)
applies compulsory education to every child aged four to sixteen living in
the Maldives. Section 15(a) requires enrolment at the age-appropriate level,
from preschool through secondary education.

[Section 10(a)](https://www.moe.gov.mv/storage/files/resources/English_translation_of_Education_Act.pdf#page=8)
requires parents and guardians:

> To enroll children of mandatory age for compulsory education to school
> and to send such children to school regularly;

Section 15(b) ends the attendance obligation where a child completes
recognised secondary education before sixteen.

## Ministerial exemption

[Section 16](https://www.moe.gov.mv/storage/files/resources/English_translation_of_Education_Act.pdf#page=11)
provides:

> (a) Notwithstanding Section 15 of this Act, the Minister has the
> discretion to exempt a child from attending school in the event the child
> does not have the ability based on the circumstances, the Regulations
> made pursuant to this Act.
>
> (b) An exception as provided for under subsection (a) of this Section
> shall only be given if doing so works for the best interest of the Child.
>
> (c) In the event an exception is granted under this Section, the Child
> still has the right to education through an educational programme
> determined by the Ministry.

## Home-based learning under the Inclusive Education Policy

The [Inclusive Education Policy (2021), section 7(h)(1)](https://ied.gov.mv/storage/uploads/7jqJbjq5/iziq8fk9.pdf#page=12),
provides for home-based learning. The English version published by the
Department of Inclusive Education defines this programme as follows:

> This is an educational program provided at home for students with complex
> learning profiles unable to attend school due to a heightened level of
> disability or due to a pandemic or a disaster or any other acceptable
> reason.

Section 6 includes pupils with disabilities, additional learning needs,
and circumstances affecting their education, including illness, abuse,
bullying and disasters.

[Section 7(b)](https://ied.gov.mv/storage/uploads/7jqJbjq5/iziq8fk9.pdf#page=8)
provides:

> Individualized Curriculum Plans (ICPs) must be developed for children
> under special circumstances and education provided accordingly.

Under sections 10(d) and 12, schools prepare and review individual plans
each term, assess pupils' learning circumstances through the referral
system, and arrange the educational programme and resources indicated by
the assessment.

## Legislative reform announced in 2026

In the [Presidential Address of 5 February 2026](https://presidency.gov.mv/Press/Article/36321),
the President announced an intended legislative amendment to establish
home-schooling based on professional assessments of a pupil's disability
or mental health condition.
