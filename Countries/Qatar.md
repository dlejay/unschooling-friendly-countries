# Unschooling in Qatar

## Compulsory education and school attendance

Article 2 of [Law No. 25 of 2001 on Compulsory Education, as amended](https://www.almeezan.qa/LawView.aspx?LawID=3973&language=ar&opt=), makes education compulsory and free from the beginning of primary education (unofficial translation from Arabic):

> until the end of the preparatory stage or reaching age eighteen, whichever is earlier

Article 3 requires enrolment of children reaching six by the end of December of the relevant school year. Separately, Article 7 requires schools to notify guardians of non-enrolment or unjustified irregular attendance; continued non-enrolment or renewed unjustified absence constitutes a breach.

Article 9 permits education at an equivalent private school or institute at the guardian's expense, with notification to the Ministry at least one week before the school year.

## Exemptions

[Article 8](https://almeezan.qa/LawArticles.aspx?LawArticleID=52892&LawID=3973&language=ar) provides exemption where illness or disability prevents study in general or special-education schools. The Minister decides on a certificate from the competent Ministry of Public Health medical authority. The same rule applies to pupils excluded from study for objective reasons. Exemption ends when its cause ends.

## Public continuing education: the home-study system

The Ministry's [registration service for home-study students (*manazil*)](https://www.sharek.gov.qa/en/articles/ministry-of-education-and-higher-education/registration-as-a-homeschool-student-in-public-schools-for-continuing-education-students/679b31bb22938a05a01e572f) covers continuing education. Its [Arabic eligibility conditions](https://www.sharek.gov.qa/en/articles/ministry-of-education-and-higher-education/%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B2%D9%84-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%B1/679a03fc6798e422f45aba19), condition 2, specify people outside compulsory education (unofficial translation):

> those over eighteen or who have completed the preparatory stage, whichever is earlier

All nationalities may register. Condition 3 also covers pupils removed from daytime education for marriage, repeated failure or employment, under ministry policies. Registration requires an attested recent academic certificate, Qatari ID, photographs and an employer's letter where applicable; foreign certificates require equivalence recognition.

## School-based home education: Doha Academy's pilot

[Doha Academy's Home Schooling Policy, reviewed June 2025, printed pp. 1–5](https://dohaacademy.sch.qa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/DA-Home-Schooling-Policy-_-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B2%D9%84%D9%8A.pdf#page=2), describes its selection for the Ministry's 2024–2025 pilot. Its eligibility criteria require British nationality, age twelve or above and eligibility for secondary education, plus a year outside school or transfer from existing home education.

Parents apply through the school and sign its agreement. The process includes a documented case study, parent-and-pupil interview, placement test and the parent's educational qualification certificate. The school registers pupils in the ministry system.

Pupils remain enrolled in their age-appropriate year group, with recorded lessons, learning resources, teacher feedback and assessed assignments. Recorded lessons permit flexible timing. Summative examinations, including ministry subjects, require attendance in person. These are the conditions of this school's programme.

## Official statistics

The Ministry's [*Statistical Highlights 2025–2026*, June 2026, p. 13](https://edu.gov.qa/ar/Content/EducationAnnualStatistics/?file=16a3dd81-e795-45c5-8735-599d8af36fa9) records **417,934 pupils** in public and private education: **148,841 public** and **269,093 private**. The table includes pre-primary education and specialised schools, excludes nurseries, adult education and Aspire Academy, and measures school enrolment.
