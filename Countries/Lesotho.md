# Home education in Lesotho

## Compulsory school enrolment and attendance

The Education Act 2010 was renamed the **Basic Education Act 2010** by the Education (Amendment) Act 2021. [2021 Act, s.1, Gazette p.751](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/ls/2021/ls-government-gazette-dated-2021-05-07-no-47.pdf).

Section 6(1) requires parents to enrol children in primary school at six, including in the year they turn six by 30 June, and to keep them in school until the age prescribed by the Minister. Section 6(2) requires attendance on each day and during each part of the day when instruction is provided. [2010 Act, s.6(1)–(2), Gazette p.165](https://www.education.gov.ls/static/media/education_act_2010.271d6a815454f460c07d.pdf).

Section 4(6) requires enrolment in an independent or public school and obliges the parent to:

> cause the learner to receive full time education appropriate for his or her age, ability and aptitude by regular attendance at school;

[2010 Act, s.4(6)(b), Gazette p.165](https://www.education.gov.ls/static/media/education_act_2010.271d6a815454f460c07d.pdf).

## Exceptions to daily attendance

Section 6(3) excuses daily attendance where a parent supplies a reason acceptable to the principal, where the principal gives written notice excluding the child because of infectious or contagious disease, or where disability or disease prevents attendance. Following exclusion by such notice, readmission requires a medical practitioner's certificate of fitness to return. [2010 Act, s.6(3)–(4), Gazette p.166](https://www.education.gov.ls/static/media/education_act_2010.271d6a815454f460c07d.pdf).

## Registered educational provision

Section 7(1) requires a school to be registered before operating. The statutory definition of a school includes formal and distance-learning posts. [2010 Act, ss.2 and 7(1)](https://www.education.gov.ls/static/media/education_act_2010.271d6a815454f460c07d.pdf).
