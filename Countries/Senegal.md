# Home education in Senegal

## Compulsory schooling

Article 3 bis of the *Loi d’orientation de l’Éducation nationale* No 91-22, inserted by Law No 2004-37 of 15 December 2004, makes schooling compulsory for children of both sexes aged six to sixteen. It provides:

> Parents whose children reach the age of six must enrol them in a public or private school.

*Unofficial English translation from French.* Parents must ensure attendance until sixteen. The State must maintain children within the school system, and compulsory schooling is free in public educational institutions. [Law No 2004-37, Article 1 inserting Article 3 bis; Official Gazette No 6202, 22 January 2005, p.52](https://www.archives.sn/api/fichiers/6253e214-c8ea-419e-8baa-12738a4a9c33#page=2).

Article 3 bis directs children under sixteen who cannot remain in general education to a vocational training institution. Article 3 of the amending law limited its transitional school-availability condition to the period ending in 2010. [Law No 2004-37, Articles 1 and 3, p.52](https://www.archives.sn/api/fichiers/6253e214-c8ea-419e-8baa-12738a4a9c33#page=2).

## Parents and educational institutions

Article 20 of the Constitution recognises parents' natural right and duty to bring up their children. Article 22 guarantees access to school and recognises religious and non-religious institutions and communities as means of education. Article 23 provides:

> Private schools may be opened with the authorisation and under the supervision of the State.

*Unofficial English translation from French.* [Constitution, Articles 20–23, Constitutional Council text](https://conseilconstitutionnel.sn/la-constitution/).

## Official statistics

The Ministry's *Rapport national sur la Situation de l’Éducation 2024* records 2,350,515 elementary-school pupils: 80.9% in public schools, 18.9% in private schools and 0.2% in community or association schools. These are school-enrolment figures. [RNSE 2024, p.103, Table 71, published through the ANSD catalogue](https://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/353/download/3169#page=104).
