# Unschooling in Hungary

English translations of the Hungarian provisions and official guidance quoted below are unofficial.

## Compulsory education and preschool attendance

Under [Act CXC of 2011, section 45(2)–(3)](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2011-190-00-00), compulsory education (*tankötelezettség*) normally starts in September for children who turn six by 31 August, with provision for an authorised one-year deferral. It ends with the school year in which the pupil turns sixteen; for special educational needs, an expert recommendation permits extension until the end of the teaching year in which the pupil turns twenty-three.

Preschool attendance is separate: [section 8(2)](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2011-190-00-00) requires at least four hours daily from the preschool year beginning after the child turns three by 31 August, subject to statutory exemptions.

## Education otherwise than through daily school attendance

[Section 45(5) of Act CXC of 2011](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2011-190-00-00) provides:

> Compulsory education may be fulfilled by attending school. If justified by the pupil's individual characteristics or particular situation, and advantageous for the pupil's development and the successful continuation and completion of their studies, an individual work arrangement may be requested for a fixed period to fulfil compulsory education.

The *egyéni munkarend* is an authorised individual study arrangement within an existing school enrolment. The [Education Authority's guidance](https://www.oktatas.hu/kozneveles/egyeni_tanuloi_munkarend) requires an active pupil–school relationship and identifies the Education Authority (*Oktatási Hivatal*) as the approving body for public-education institutions. Vocational institutions have a separate *egyéni tanulmányi rend*, authorised by the headteacher under vocational-education rules.

## Application and authorisation

The [Education Authority's guidance](https://www.oktatas.hu/kozneveles/egyeni_tanuloi_munkarend) specifies that a parent or guardian applies for a minor; an adult pupil applies personally. Applications are normally due by 15 June before the relevant school year. Later applications require a circumstance preventing fulfilment of compulsory education through school attendance.

The application identifies the pupil, school, year group and requested duration, and explains the individual circumstances and educational benefit. It may be submitted electronically or by post. Supporting expert or medical documentation is required for the corresponding special-needs or long-term-treatment grounds. Where parents exercise custody jointly, the applicant declares the other parent's agreement.

The Authority's [FAQ, “From when is the pupil no longer required to attend school activities?”](https://www.oktatas.hu/kozneveles/egyeni_tanuloi_munkarend/gyik), states:

> The pupil must attend school lessons and school activities until receipt of the decision authorising the individual work arrangement.

Authorisation is for a fixed period and a specified school. Expiry requires a new application; changing schools invalidates the existing authorisation. These rules are set out in the same official FAQ.

## Curriculum, preparation and assessment

[Decree 20/2012 (VIII. 31.) EMMI, section 75(1)](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2012-20-20-5H), provides:

> If the pupil fulfils their study obligation under an individual work arrangement at the parent's request, the parent is responsible for their preparation, and the pupil prepares individually.

Under section 75(2)–(3), the school is responsible for preparation and assessment where the arrangement rests on the relevant expert opinion concerning special educational needs or learning, behavioural or adjustment difficulties, or on qualifying long-term medical treatment.

The school's curriculum remains applicable. [Section 7/A(1) of the same decree](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2012-20-20-5H) states:

> The school incorporates the framework curricula into its local curriculum, organised by school year, and supplements them according to the school's local characteristics.

[Section 64(2)(a)](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2012-20-20-5H) requires:

> The pupil must take a classification examination to establish the mid-year and end-of-year grade if […] they have been exempted from attending lessons (except pupils receiving developmental education).

The [Education Authority's FAQ](https://www.oktatas.hu/kozneveles/egyeni_tanuloi_munkarend/gyik) specifies examinations in every subject under the school's pedagogical programme. Individual work arrangements do not themselves exempt pupils from subject requirements; separate headteacher exemptions may apply to skills subjects. For pupils living abroad, the headteacher may waive mid-year examinations, with the whole year's material assessed at year-end.
