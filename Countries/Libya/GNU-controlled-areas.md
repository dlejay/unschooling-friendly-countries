# Unschooling in GNU-controlled areas of Libya

## Administration

In areas where the Tripoli-based Government of National Unity (GNU) exercises
effective civilian authority, education is administered by the GNU Ministry of
Education through municipal education monitors, the National Examinations Centre
and the ministry's central departments.

For example, GNU Ministerial Decision No. 1077 of 2025 set the teaching and
examination calendar for the 2025–2026 school year, including a school start date
of 21 September 2025.

## Compulsory education

Basic education is compulsory for nine years.

## Home education

The applicable GNU regulation is Decision No. 1013 of 2022. Article 58 limits
home education to pupils who:

- have special needs;
- live abroad in a country without a Libyan school; or
- cannot enrol in an educational institution because of special circumstances.

Admission in the third category requires approval from the Basic Education
Administration. Articles 59 and 60 regulate the minimum entry age, registration,
assignment to an approved school and recording of results through the National
Examinations Centre.
