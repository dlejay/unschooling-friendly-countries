# Unschooling in contested or overlapping areas of Libya

## Scope

This profile covers localities where current military control does not establish
which education administration handles school registration, home-education
applications and examinations. This situation is particularly relevant in parts
of southern Libya, but the scope is evidence-based rather than a fixed boundary
around Fezzan.

Security may be exercised by the LAAF, an LAAF-aligned unit or a local armed
group while the local education monitor continues to participate in GNU
programmes, responds to the GNS Ministry of Education, or interacts with both.

## Evidence of overlapping administration

- In February 2025, a committee appointed by the GNS education minister met the
  education monitors for Sebha and neighbouring southern municipalities.
- In July 2024, a GNS education delegation inspected the education monitor in
  Ubari, including its examination and private-education work.
- In July 2025, an official GNU Ministry of Education programme included pupils
  and schools represented through the Sebha and Ubari education monitors.

These facts demonstrate administrative contact with both rival ministries. They
do not establish that both ministries' regulations are applied simultaneously
to every family in those municipalities.
