## Context

Libya has two principal rival centres of power: the Tripoli-based Government of
National Unity (GNU) in the west and the Libyan Arab Armed Forces (LAAF) and its
associated House of Representatives-appointed Government of National Stability
(GNS) in the east and much of the south.

Military control does not always identify the education administration that a
family encounters. In parts of southern Libya, local education monitors have
interacted with institutions associated with both rival governments.

## Common legal background

Decision No. 211 of 2011 recognises home education as a form of non-state
education. It requires registration through a designated school, a resident
family member with at least an intermediate qualification, use of the prescribed
curriculum and assessment under the school-examination rules.

The GNU subsequently adopted Decision No. 1013 of 2022. Its more recent
home-education provisions restrict access to specified categories of pupils.
Whether the GNS applies that GNU decision has not been established.
