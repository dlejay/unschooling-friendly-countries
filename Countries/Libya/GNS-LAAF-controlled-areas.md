# Unschooling in GNS/LAAF-controlled areas of Libya

## Administration

The Libyan Arab Armed Forces (LAAF) exercise military control across eastern
Libya and parts of southern Libya. Civilian education is administered by the
House of Representatives-appointed Government of National Stability (GNS),
which has its own Ministry of Education and works through local education
monitors.

This administration is operational rather than merely claimed. The GNS education
minister has met the Benghazi education monitor, and GNS education delegations
have worked directly with education monitors in southern municipalities,
including Sebha, Ubari and Traghen.

## Compulsory education

Basic education is compulsory for nine years under Libya's national education
framework.

## Home education

No primary source has yet been found showing that the GNS adopted or applies GNU
Decision No. 1013 of 2022.

The confirmed pre-division baseline is Decision No. 211 of 2011. Under its home-
education provisions:

- a resident family member must hold at least an intermediate qualification;
- the pupil must register through a designated school;
- the school supplies the prescribed books and curriculum; and
- the pupil is assessed under the applicable school-examination rules.
