# Unschooling in Angola

## Compulsory education

Compulsory education in Angola covers 10 years:

- the *Classe de Iniciação*, normally attended at age 5;
- six years of primary education;
- and the first cycle of secondary education, comprising grades 7–9.

Article 12 of the *Lei de Bases do Sistema de Educação e Ensino*, as amended and republished by *Lei n.º 32/20*, provides:

> **Article 12 — Obligatoriness**
>
> 1. The obligation of Education translates into the duty of the State, society, families and companies to ensure and promote access to and attendance in the Education and Teaching System for all individuals of school age.
> 2. The obligation of Education covers the Initiation Class, Primary Education and the First Cycle of Secondary Education.

## Home education

The *Lei de Bases do Sistema de Educação e Ensino* does not provide for *ensino doméstico* or an equivalent form of parent-directed home education.

The differentiated forms of education identified in the legislation include special education, extra-school education, distance education and semi-presential education.

The *Regime Jurídico do Ensino Primário e Secundário*, adopted by *Decreto Presidencial n.º 162/23*, regulates primary and secondary education through enrolment in educational institutions. It provides for enrolment in the first grade from age 6 and regulates attendance and absences.

Distance education is provided through authorised educational institutions and follows an approved educational programme. Under *Decreto Presidencial n.º 321/20*, the ordinary model combines 70–75% non-presential activities with 25–30% presencial activities, subject to authorised variations.

The national curriculum applies to primary and secondary education and defines the subjects, educational objectives and curricular organisation applicable at each level.
