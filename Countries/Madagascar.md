# Unschooling in Madagascar

## Constitutional framework

Article 23 of the Constitution recognises every child's right to instruction
and education under parental responsibility, with respect for the parents'
freedom of choice. Article 24 requires the State to organise free public
education accessible to all and makes primary education compulsory for all.
Article 25 recognises private education, subject to equivalence of teaching
conditions concerning hygiene, morality and educational standards prescribed
by law.

## Compulsory education

Law No. 2022-018 of 30 January 2023, on the general orientation of the education
system, establishes compulsory education from the age of 5 (Article 15).
Article 48 prescribes one compulsory year of preparation for primary school.
Primary education comprises two compulsory cycles of three years each
(Articles 51–53).

Lower-secondary education lasts three years (Article 55). Article 56 makes
this cycle compulsory but expressly provides for progressive implementation
according to the human, material and financial resources of each municipality.
The implementing arrangements are to be specified by regulation.

## Educational provision

Article 46 defines formal education as education delivered within a school
framework. Article 49 provides for preschool in public or private educational
establishments. Article 51 states expressly that primary education is delivered
in public or private primary schools.
Article 54 likewise places secondary education in public or private secondary
schools. Article 106 includes regular attendance among learners' obligations.

Article 84 defines non-formal education as learning outside a school framework
and states that it is to enable people outside school age to acquire
knowledge, vocational skills, general culture and civic aptitudes. Articles
85–87 provide for literacy centres, specialised training centres and other
training structures. Their programmes must meet the aims of primary education,
except for basic vocational training. Article 92 separately opens citizenship
and civic education to people of all ages.

## Private education

Article 117 requires prior authorisation to establish a private educational
institution. Article 118 requires teachers in such institutions to hold the
prescribed academic or pedagogical qualifications and obtain authorisation
to teach.

Under Article 120, private institutions must apply the official curricula and
teaching hours. They may add content reflecting their specific character.
Article 121 permits institutions with particular curricula and study
arrangements following express authorisation from the competent ministry.

## Foreign families

Article 1 of Law No. 2022-018 applies to educational activities organised within
the territory of the Republic and to public and private educational
institutions. Its compulsory-education provisions contain no distinction based
on nationality.
