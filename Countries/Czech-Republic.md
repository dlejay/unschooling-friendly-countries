# Unschooling in the Czech Republic

## Compulsory education

Sections 36(1) and 36(3) of the [Education Act, No. 561/2004
Coll.](https://ppropo.mpsv.cz/zakon_561_2004) provide:

> (1) School attendance is compulsory for nine school years, but no later
> than the end of the school year in which the pupil reaches the age of
> seventeen (hereinafter referred to as "compulsory school attendance").
>
> […]
>
> (3) Compulsory school attendance begins at the start of the school year
> following the day on which the child reaches the age of six, unless a
> postponement has been authorised. […]

Section 34(1) also establishes compulsory preschool education:

> From the start of the school year following the day on which the child
> reaches the age of five until the commencement of the child's compulsory
> school attendance, preschool education is compulsory, unless otherwise
> provided below.

## Home education and authorisation

Home education is regulated as *individual education* (*individuální
vzdělávání*). It is available at both the first and second stages of basic
school, covering primary and lower secondary education.

Sections 41(1) and 41(3) provide:

> (1) Permission for a pupil's individual education shall be decided by the
> headteacher of the school to which the pupil has been admitted to fulfil
> compulsory school attendance, on the basis of a written application from
> the pupil's legal representative.
>
> […]
>
> (3) The headteacher shall permit individual education if:
>
> a) there are serious reasons for individual education;
>
> b) adequate conditions for individual education are ensured, particularly
> material conditions and protection of the pupil's health;
>
> c) the person who will educate the pupil has obtained at least secondary
> education with a school-leaving examination (*maturita*), and, where the
> pupil is at the second stage of basic school, university education;
>
> d) suitable textbooks and teaching texts according to which the pupil is
> to be educated are provided.

Section 41(2) requires the application to identify the pupil and the proposed
period of individual education, give reasons, describe the premises,
resources and health-protection arrangements, document the educator's
qualifications, and include a statement from an educational counselling
facility. It must also list teaching texts without Ministry approval and
other facts affecting the pupil's education.

## Educational programme and assessment

The [Public Administration Portal](https://portal.gov.cz/informace/povinna-skolni-dochazka-INF-274)
specifies that individual education follows the educational programme of
the school in which the pupil is enrolled. Section 5(1) of the Act provides:

> The school educational programme for education for which a framework
> educational programme is issued under section 3(1) must comply with that
> framework educational programme; the educational content may be organised
> in the school educational programme into subjects or other integrated
> units of study (for example, modules).

Sections 41(5) and 41(6) govern assessment:

> (5) An individually educated pupil shall take examinations for each
> half-year on the relevant subject matter, at the school to which the
> pupil has been admitted to fulfil compulsory school attendance.
>
> (6) If an individually educated pupil cannot be assessed at the end of
> the relevant half-year, the headteacher shall set an alternative date
> for assessment, so that the assessment is completed no later than two
> months after the end of the half-year.

## Termination and inspection

Section 41(8) provides:

> The headteacher shall terminate individual education:
>
> a) if adequate conditions for education are not ensured, particularly
> material and staffing conditions and protection of the pupil's health;
>
> b) if the legal representative fails to fulfil the conditions for
> individual education laid down by this Act;
>
> c) if the pupil fails at the end of the first or second half-year of
> the relevant school year;
>
> d) if the pupil cannot be assessed in the manner specified in
> paragraphs 5 and 6; or
>
> e) at the request of the pupil's legal representative.

Under section 41(9), termination entails placement in the appropriate
grade of basic school. An appeal does not suspend that decision.

Section 174(2) extends the Czech School Inspectorate's powers to places
where individual education is provided. Section 174(3) provides:

> If the place where a pupil's individual education authorised under
> section 41(3) takes place is a dwelling, and the person living there does
> not consent to entry, the inspection under paragraph 2 shall take place
> at the school to which the pupil was admitted to fulfil school attendance.
> In this case, the Czech School Inspectorate shall agree the inspection
> date with the pupil's legal representative, where possible. The inspection
> shall also take place in this case in the presence and with the cooperation
> of the person educating the pupil under section 41(3)(c).

## Individual preschool education

Preschool individual education has a separate notification procedure.
Section 34b(1) provides:

> The legal representative of a child for whom preschool education is
> compulsory may, in justified cases, choose individual education for the
> child. If the child is to be individually educated for most of the school
> year, the child's legal representative must give this notification no
> later than three months before the start of the school year. During the
> school year, the obligation of individual preschool education may be
> fulfilled no earlier than the day on which the notification of the child's
> individual education is delivered to the headteacher of the kindergarten
> to which the child has been admitted for preschool education.

Section 34b(3) provides:

> The kindergarten headteacher shall recommend to the legal representative
> of an individually educated child the areas in which the child is to be
> educated. These areas are based on the framework educational programme
> for preschool education. […]

The same paragraph requires the kindergarten to verify the child's progress
in the expected outcomes during the third or fourth month of the school
year. The legal representative must ensure the child's participation.
Under section 34b(4), failure to attend even on the alternative date entails
termination of individual education.

The Ministry's [November 2025 guidance, section 4](https://edu.gov.cz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Metodicke_doporuceni_k_individualnimu_vzdelavani_deti_v_materskych_skolach.pdf)
specifies:

> Any shortcomings identified during verification are not grounds for
> terminating individual education […]

## Foreign families

Section 36(2) defines the scope of compulsory school attendance:

> Compulsory school attendance applies to citizens of the Czech Republic
> and to citizens of another Member State of the European Union who stay
> in the Czech Republic for more than 90 days. Compulsory school attendance
> also applies to other foreign nationals who are entitled to reside in
> the Czech Republic permanently or temporarily for more than 90 days,
> and to participants in proceedings for the granting of international
> protection.

Section 34a(1) applies the same categories of foreign nationals to compulsory
preschool education. Section 41(2)(a) provides for the pupil's residential
address when the pupil has no permanent residence address in the Czech
Republic.
