# Unschooling in Togo

The quotations below are unofficial English translations of the French texts.

## Compulsory schooling

Article 16 of the Solemn Declaration of Fundamental Rights and Duties of
Persons and Citizens, annexed to the [Constitution of 6 May 2024](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_06_05_2024%20-%2069%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B042%20BIS%20.pdf#page=17),
provides:

> Everyone has the right to education.
>
> Education aims at the full development of the human personality, with
> respect for democratic principles of communal life and fundamental rights.
> School attendance is compulsory for children of both sexes until the age
> of fifteen.
>
> The State shall progressively ensure free public education.

The Constitution's preamble makes this Declaration an integral part of the
Constitution.

[Article 255 of the Children's Code](https://creusetogo.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/CODE-TOGOLAIS-DE-LENFANT.pdf#page=23),
enacted by Law No. 2007-017 of 6 July 2007, also provides:

> School attendance is compulsory for children of both sexes until the age
> of fifteen (15).
>
> The State shall progressively ensure free public education.
>
> The State shall ensure the quality of education.

## Parental rights and responsibilities

[Article 15 of the constitutional Declaration](https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_06_05_2024%20-%2069%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B042%20BIS%20.pdf#page=17)
provides:

> The maintenance and education of children are a right of parents and an
> obligation that falls primarily upon them. They are supported by the State
> in this task.

[Article 253 of the Children's Code](https://creusetogo.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/CODE-TOGOLAIS-DE-LENFANT.pdf#page=23)
provides:

> Parents and, where applicable, legal guardians or members of the extended
> family have the prior right to:
>
> a. choose the kind of education to be given to their children;
>
> b. choose a school other than those established by the public authorities,
> provided that it meets the minimum standards prescribed by the State;
>
> c. provide their children's moral education in accordance with their
> convictions and in the children's best interests;
>
> d. guide, advise and direct the child in a manner consistent with the
> development of the child's capacities.
