# Unschooling in Guinea

## Compulsory education

Under the Constitution adopted in 2025, children must have access to school by
the age of 5 at the latest and must remain in school until at least the age of
17.

Article 21 provides that the State guarantees free education, "compulsory
access" to school by age 5 at the latest, and children's continued attendance
at school until at least age 17.

Older sources describing compulsory education in Guinea as lasting nine years,
generally from ages 7 to 16, therefore no longer reflect the current
constitutional rule.

## Unschooling

There is no identified legal framework allowing parents to satisfy compulsory
schooling through independent home education.

UNESCO's country profile for Guinea found no provisions concerning
homeschooling. The current Constitution is also framed expressly in terms of
children entering and remaining “at school”, rather than merely receiving an
education by some alternative means.

## Statistics

According to UNICEF's 2023 annual report, 34% of primary-school-age children
and 47% of lower-secondary-school-age children were out of school.

## News

A new Constitution was approved by referendum on 21 September 2025 and
promulgated on 26 September 2025, ending the constitutional transition that
followed the 2021 military takeover.

Mamadi Doumbouya subsequently won the presidential election held on
28 December 2025 with 86.72% of the vote according to the final results, and
was inaugurated as President on 17 January 2026.
