# Unschooling in Mongolia

All quotations below are unofficial translations from Mongolian.

## Compulsory education

Article 40.2.2 of the [General Law on Education of 7 July 2023](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16759958962301)
requires parents, guardians and custodians to:

> Ensure that children participate in compulsory pre-school education at
> the age of five and in general education thereafter.

Article 3.1.1 defines general education as:

> Education acquired over a total period of twelve years, comprising
> primary, basic and complete secondary education levels.

Article 10.2.5 requires learners to acquire general education. Under
article 4.1 of the [Law on Pre-school and General Education of 7 July
2023](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16759959012931), primary education
comprises five years, basic education nine years and complete secondary
education twelve years, counted cumulatively.

Article 4.3 provides:

> Five-year-old children must receive pre-school education and participate
> in at least 200 hours of instruction.

Article 4.5 provides:

> Children who turn six in the relevant year shall be enrolled in a
> general education school.

Article 4.6 permits enrolment at age seven for developmental, health or
other justified reasons. In that case, article 4.7 requires parents,
guardians and custodians to support the child's development in accordance
with directions from the school administration.

## Home education at primary level

[Article 22.7 of the Law on Pre-school and General Education](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16759959012931)
provides:

> At the request of parents, guardians and custodians, primary education
> may be provided through home education. The procedure for organising
> and supervising home education shall be approved by the member of the
> Government responsible for education.

The primary-school structure specified in article 20.2.1 comprises
grades 1–5.

## Other forms of instruction

[Articles 8.1 and 8.2 of the General Law on Education](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16759958962301)
provide:

> 8.1. Instruction shall be organised in classroom, non-classroom or
> combined forms.
>
> 8.2. Non-classroom instruction includes distance, alternative, online
> and practical instruction.

[Article 22.6 of the Law on Pre-school and General Education](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16759959012931)
provides:

> General education schools shall organise distance and online instruction
> in accordance with the procedure specified in article 26.1.11 of the
> General Law on Education.

Article 8.3 of the General Law on Education permits individual instruction
for learners with disabilities at the request and with the consent of
their parents, guardians or custodians. Article 6.3 of the Law on
Pre-school and General Education provides:

> Kindergartens and general education schools shall develop individual
> learning plans for learners with differing needs, based on the views
> and consent of parents, guardians and custodians, and shall implement
> them with their participation and support.

## Equivalency programmes

The [Regulation on Instruction through Equivalency Programmes for Primary,
Basic and Complete Secondary Education](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=208413),
annexed to Ministerial Order A/168 of 3 April 2018, provides in clause 1.4:

> Persons who have never attended school or who have been out of school
> for two or more years shall be enrolled in equivalency-programme
> instruction.

Clause 4.3 provides:

> Depending on the learner's level of knowledge and skills and
> opportunities for learning, instruction shall be organised through
> classroom, distance, independent-study, home or tutorial instruction,
> short-term gatherings, or a combination of these forms, observing the
> duration prescribed in clause 4.2.

Clause 4.2 sets the duration at no less than 70 per cent of the approved
primary, basic and complete secondary education teaching plan.
Clause 4.4 provides, in part:

> Where a learner enters into an agreement to study in a non-classroom
> form, one third of the scheduled hours specified in clause 4.2 must
> nevertheless be completed through classroom instruction [...].

Under clause 3.4, learners seeking recognition of independently acquired
knowledge and skills submit a request to the Education Department through
the provincial or district Lifelong Education Centre or unit. Clause 3.5
provides for the opening of a learner's file and recording of the
corresponding grade-level assessment on the basis of the Department's
decision for learners who have never attended school.

## Curriculum and assessment

[Article 9.2 of the Law on Pre-school and General Education](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16759959012931)
provides:

> The content of general education shall be determined with a view to
> developing knowledge and skills in mathematics, natural sciences,
> social sciences and humanities, language and communication, physical
> education, arts and aesthetics, digital and design technology, civic
> education and life skills.

Article 10.4 assigns approval of the curricula and general education
teaching plan to the member of the Government responsible for education,
on the basis of the Professional Council's conclusions and recommendations.
Article 10.7 permits kindergartens and schools to adapt the curriculum to
learners' differing needs with parental participation.

Article 27.2 provides:

> Learners shall be assessed during instruction, on promotion to the next
> grade and on graduation.

Under article 27.4, schools organise grade-promotion assessments and local
education authorities organise school-graduation assessments. Article
27.5 provides for educational credentials to be issued on the basis of
the graduation assessment.
