# Home education in Georgia

## Compulsory education

Article 9(1) of the *Law on General Education* makes primary and basic education compulsory. Under the current Article 2(ო), these comprise grades I–VI and VII–IX respectively. School entry is normally at six, under Article 6(6); Article 63¹² permits voluntary entry in 2026–2027 for children turning six between 16 September and 31 December 2026. [Law, current Georgian consolidation, Articles 2, 6, 9 and 63¹²](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/29248?publication=118).

## Legal route outside school

Article 9(4) provides:

> General education may be acquired through external studies.

*Unofficial English translation from Georgian.* Article 2(კ) defines external studies by reference to certification of independently acquired general education programmes. Article 9(4¹) assigns the conditions and procedure to the Ministry. [Law, Articles 2(კ) and 9(4)–(4¹)](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/29248?publication=118).

## Registration and eligibility

The Ministry's notice of 18 February 2026 requires candidates to be at least the age of the relevant grade's cohort and to have completed the preceding grade's programme. An adult applicant or a minor's legal representative submits the registration application during the prescribed period to an educational resource centre. For a candidate enrolled at school, the responsible centre is the one serving that school; a candidate outside school may choose any centre.

The application identifies the grades, semesters or subjects, examination language, foreign languages and elective subject. Supporting documents include identification and proof of preceding studies, with recognition of foreign education where applicable. [Ministry, external-examination registration notice, 18 February 2026](https://mes.gov.ge/content.php?id=14495&lang=geo).

## Curriculum and assessment

Article 9(4) conditions the state education certificate on achieving the National Curriculum's attainment level. Article 2(ვ) defines that curriculum as determining learning objectives, outcomes, teaching content and subject-hour allocations. [Law, Articles 2(ვ) and 9(4)](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/29248?publication=118).

Order No 122/ნ of 10 September 2026 added Article 7(3¹) to the external-certification regulation:

> […] the school may conduct a combined subject examination allowing each grade to be assessed separately.

*Unofficial English translation from Georgian.* This applies when two or more primary or basic grades in the same subject are examined together. The amended Article 13(3) uses pass/fail assessment for sports-group subjects and electives from the second semester of grade V through grade XII. [Order No 122/ნ, Article 1](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/6997960).

## Official statistics

Geostat reports approximately 644,200 pupils in general education institutions at the beginning of 2025–2026, including 88.8% in public schools and 11.2% in private schools. These figures measure institutional enrolment. [Geostat, release of 2 December 2025, p.2 (PDF p.3)](https://geostat.ge/media/75468/Indicators-of-Secondary-General-Education-Schools---2025-2026.pdf#page=3).
