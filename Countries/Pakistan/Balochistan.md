# Home education in Balochistan

## Compulsory education

The *Balochistan Compulsory Education Act 2014* concerns children aged five to sixteen. Section 3 requires school attendance, subject to a reasonable excuse, until completion of the highest education course offered in the school. Section 1(2) extends the Act throughout Balochistan except the Tribal Areas. [Act 2014, sections 1–3, PDF pp.2–3](https://balochistancode.gob.pk/lawdir/3a78cfa3-fce8-4e76-9d3c-00992080db78.pdf#page=2).

## Education outside school and oversight

Section 6(b) recognises a child receiving:

> otherwise than in a school, instruction which in the opinion of the Prescribed Authority, is sufficient

The exception concerns instruction being received outside school; its standard is sufficiency in the opinion of the provincially notified Prescribed Authority. Under section 5, a management committee may issue an attendance order after giving the parent an opportunity to be heard and making the inquiries it considers necessary. [Act 2014, sections 2(k), 5 and 6(b), PDF pp.3 and 5](https://balochistancode.gob.pk/lawdir/3a78cfa3-fce8-4e76-9d3c-00992080db78.pdf#page=5).

## Official statistics

PIE records 751 non-formal education centres and 38,115 learners in Balochistan in 2024–2025. These are programme enrolments. [NFE Annual Statistical Report 2024–25, p.16, Table 1](https://pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/NFE%202024-25.pdf#page=21).
