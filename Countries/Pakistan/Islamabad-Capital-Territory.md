# Home education in Islamabad Capital Territory

## Compulsory education and school attendance

The *Right to Free and Compulsory Education Act 2012* applies to Islamabad Capital Territory and covers children aged five to sixteen. Section 8(1) requires a parent, subject to a reasonable excuse, to:

> cause a child to attend a school in the neighbourhood until the said child has completed the prescribed education.

Section 8(2) specifies excuses including a school management committee's satisfaction that infirmity or mental incapacity prevents attendance, that further study should not be compelled, or that no school exists in the neighbourhood. [Act 2012, sections 1–2 and 8, The Islamabad Laws, pp.827 and 831](https://www.cda.gov.pk/public/Assets/pdf/1699885321_26bb7cbe_2f92_4e9f_9dbf_9a6e6defde10_.pdf#page=845).

## Curriculum and administration

Section 7(3) assigns curriculum and evaluation procedures, including syllabuses and textbook approval, to an academic authority established or specified by the Federal Government. Section 16(4) allows the school management committee to issue an attendance order after hearing the parent and making the inquiries it considers necessary. [Act 2012, sections 7(3) and 16(4), pp.831 and 835](https://www.cda.gov.pk/public/Assets/pdf/1699885321_26bb7cbe_2f92_4e9f_9a6e6defde10_.pdf#page=845).

## Official statistics

PIE records 1,586 non-formal education centres and 49,788 learners in Islamabad Capital Territory in 2024–2025. These are programme enrolments. [NFE Annual Statistical Report 2024–25, p.16, Table 1](https://pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/NFE%202024-25.pdf#page=21).
