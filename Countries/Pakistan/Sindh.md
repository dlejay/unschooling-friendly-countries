# Home education in Sindh

## Compulsory education and school attendance

The *Sindh Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act 2013* covers children aged five to sixteen. Section 8(1) requires parents or guardians, subject to a reasonable excuse, to:

> cause a child to attend a school until the said child has completed the prescribed education.

Section 8(2) specifies excuses including a school management committee's satisfaction that infirmity or lack of capacity prevents attendance, that further study should not be compelled, or that no school is within reasonable distance. [Act 2013, sections 2, 3 and 8, pp.1–2 and 4](https://rsusindh.gov.pk/contents/Sindh%20Act%20No.XIV%20of%202013.pdf#page=4).

## Educational standard and administration

Section 7(4) requires the government to ensure admission and attendance, prescribe the curriculum and courses, and maintain educational quality. Section 20 assigns the curriculum and evaluation procedure to the appointed academic authority, including comprehensive and continuous evaluation of children's understanding and ability to apply knowledge. [Act 2013, sections 7(4) and 20, pp.4 and 8](https://rsusindh.gov.pk/contents/Sindh%20Act%20No.XIV%20of%202013.pdf#page=8).

## Official statistics

PIE records 1,152 non-formal education centres and 58,411 learners in Sindh in 2024–2025. These are programme enrolments. [NFE Annual Statistical Report 2024–25, p.16, Table 1](https://pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/NFE%202024-25.pdf#page=21).
