# Home education in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

## Compulsory education

Section 3 of the *Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Free Compulsory Primary and Secondary Education Act 2017* requires school attendance until secondary education is completed, subject to the reasonable excuses in section 4. The Act concerns ages five to sixteen. Section 1(2) provides for its territorial extension to districts specified by government notification. [Act 2017, sections 1–3](https://www.kpcode.kp.gov.pk/homepage/lawDetails/1325).

## Education outside school and oversight

Section 4(b) recognises as a reasonable excuse:

> the child is receiving education otherwise than in school, which in the opinion of the School Attendance Authority are sufficient

The exception concerns education being received outside school; its standard is sufficiency in the Authority's opinion. Section 5 permits the government to constitute an Authority for each school, with mandatory parent–teacher council representation. Before making an attendance order, the Authority must give parents an opportunity to be heard and conduct such inquiries as it considers necessary. [Act 2017, sections 4(b) and 5](https://www.kpcode.kp.gov.pk/homepage/lawDetails/1325).

## Official statistics

PIE records 5,026 non-formal education centres and 352,535 learners in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in 2024–2025. These are programme enrolments. [NFE Annual Statistical Report 2024–25, p.16, Table 1](https://pie.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/NFE%202024-25.pdf#page=21).
