# Unschooling in São Tomé and Príncipe

## Compulsory education

Under *Lei n.º 4/2018*, pre-primary education is compulsory at ages 4–5. Basic education is universal, compulsory and free for nine years (grades 1–9), corresponding in Annex I to approximately ages 6–14. Article 76 states that the duration of compulsory schooling is to be regulated by a separate diploma.

Secondary education covers grades 10–12 and is universal, but is not made compulsory by the law itself. The State is required to promote conditions for extending compulsory schooling through grade 12, with the applicable regime to be established separately.

## Home education

*Lei n.º 11/93* does not authorize home education. Instead, it excludes *ensino individual* and *ensino doméstico* from the scope of the law governing private and cooperative education, and defines *ensino doméstico* as education provided in the pupil's home by a family member or another person living with the pupil.

This definition establishes what the term means for that exclusion; it does not establish that home education satisfies compulsory schooling. No current provision or procedure was identified that provides a general route for children of compulsory-school age to be educated at home.

*Lei n.º 4/2018* repealed *Lei n.º 2/2003* and any legislation contrary to it, while providing for *Lei n.º 11/93* to be amended by decree-law.
