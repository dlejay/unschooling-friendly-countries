# Unschooling in Niger

## Compulsory schooling

Primary schooling is compulsory within available school capacity under Decree No. 62-021/MEN of 7 February 1962. Article 1 applies the obligation to children of school age living in each primary school's recruitment area, up to the capacity of its entry classes. Under Article 2, where enrolments leave places available, a local commission determines which additional children must attend. [Decree, Articles 1–3, Official Gazette of 15 February 1962, p.43](https://portail.education.gouv.ne/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Decret-N%C2%B0-1962-021-MEN-du-7-fevrier-1962-portant-reglementation-de-lobligation-et-de-la-frequentation-scolaires.pdf#page=1).

The 1998 Education Framework Law (*Loi d’Orientation du Système Éducatif Nigérien*, LOSEN) guarantees education to children aged four to eighteen under Article 2. Article 19 provides for primary admission at six to seven and a normal six-year primary cycle. The four-to-eighteen guarantee is distinct from the decree's schooling obligation. [Law No. 98-12 of 1 June 1998, Articles 2 and 19, PDF pp.2 and 4](https://archives.au.int/bitstream/handle/123456789/1470/Projet_Loi_Orientation_Systeme_Educatif_Nigerien_F.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).

Article 7 of the decree states, in translation, that “regular attendance is compulsory for all enrolled pupils”, unless the education or health service declares them unfit. Articles 4–5 and 8–9 provide warnings and penalties for failure to attend without a legitimate reason or valid excuse. [Decree, Articles 4–9, Official Gazette pp.43–44](https://portail.education.gouv.ne/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Decret-N%C2%B0-1962-021-MEN-du-7-fevrier-1962-portant-reglementation-de-lobligation-et-de-la-frequentation-scolaires.pdf).

## Home education and informal learning

LOSEN Articles 39–40 recognise informal education through everyday experience and relationships, including learning within the family and community. These provisions describe forms of learning ; they do not grant an exemption from compulsory school enrolment and attendance. Article 77 repeals earlier provisions only where inconsistent with LOSEN, without expressly repealing the 1962 decree. [LOSEN, Articles 39–40 and 77, PDF pp.6 and 9](https://archives.au.int/bitstream/handle/123456789/1470/Projet_Loi_Orientation_Systeme_Educatif_Nigerien_F.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).

The decree's exceptions concern incapacity, legitimate reasons and administratively accepted excuses. Limited school capacity and such exceptions do not establish a general parental right to substitute autonomous home education for schooling. [Decree, Articles 1–2, 4, 7 and 9](https://portail.education.gouv.ne/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Decret-N%C2%B0-1962-021-MEN-du-7-fevrier-1962-portant-reglementation-de-lobligation-et-de-la-frequentation-scolaires.pdf).

## Recent developments

**2026–2027.** In its school-opening message of 30 September 2026, the Ministry announced continued support for families in areas affected by insecurity through receiving schools, regrouping centres, distance teaching and digital coaching. These measures support continuity of education for affected pupils. [Ministry of Education — school-opening message](https://info.education.gouv.ne/2026/09/30/message-de-madame-la-ministre-de-leducation-nationale-de-lalphabetisation-et-de-la-promotion-des-langues-a-loccasion-de-la-rentree-scolaire-2026-2027/).

## Statistics

For the **2026–2027** reopening, the Ministry announced an expected intake of approximately **four million pupils** across preschool, primary and secondary education, in addition to learners in literacy and non-formal education centres. This is a school-opening estimate. [Ministry message, 30 September 2026](https://info.education.gouv.ne/2026/09/30/message-de-madame-la-ministre-de-leducation-nationale-de-lalphabetisation-et-de-la-promotion-des-langues-a-loccasion-de-la-rentree-scolaire-2026-2027/).

The *2024–2025 Education Statistical Yearbook* gives the following recorded enrolments :

| School year | Primary | Secondary, both cycles |
| ----------- | ------: | ---------------------: |
| 2024–2025 | 3,037,661 | 724,423 |
| 2023–2024 | 3,002,551 | 789,075 |

The 2024–2025 census reports coverage of 97.9% of primary establishments and 97.7% of secondary establishments. These figures concern pupils reported by schools, not independently home-educated children. [Yearbook, Tables 3–4, 16 and 28, printed pp.20–21, 28 and 34](https://info.education.gouv.ne/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MEN-2025Annuaire_MENAPL_2024_2025_version_finale_valide.pdf#page=30).
