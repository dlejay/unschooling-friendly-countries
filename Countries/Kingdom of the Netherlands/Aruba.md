# Unschooling in Aruba

## Compulsory schooling

Articles 1–2 of the *Leerplichtverordening* (AB 2011 no. 82) require resident children to be enrolled in school and attend during regular school hours. Enrolment begins at the start of the school year for children already aged four or reaching that age before 1 October. The obligation ends on completing vocational or general secondary education, or on reaching 17. For children arriving after the first compulsory school day, the enrolment obligation begins 30 days after arrival. [Articles 1–2](https://cuatro.sim-cdn.nl/arubaoverheid2858bd/uploads/1304ab11.082.pdf#page=1).

## Home education

### Eligibility

Article 3(1) permits exemption from school enrolment for physical or psychological reasons, enrolment at a recognised foreign institution, or special circumstances making school enrolment undesirable. Under articles 3(4)–(5), the Minister decides within 30 days; refusals must give reasons. Exemptions under the latter two grounds last until the end of the school year. [Article 3](https://cuatro.sim-cdn.nl/arubaoverheid2858bd/uploads/1304ab11.082.pdf#page=2).

The ministerially approved June 2020 policy interprets special circumstances to include religious, pedagogical or didactic reasons for choosing an educational pathway unavailable in public or publicly funded private schools. Parents may provide alternative education themselves or through a third party. [Policy, section A.2, pp.1–2](https://www.ea.aw/pages/wp-content/uploads/l/leerplicht/Beleid-bijzondere-omstandigheden-in-kader-van-leerplicht-2020-06.pdf#page=1).

### Approval and supervision

The published procedure requires a written recognition request to Bureau Leerplicht Aruba, identifying the children and enclosing the instructor's certificate of good conduct. The Inspectorate assesses the proposed programme. Applications must be renewed before each school year, and compulsory-schooling officers check that home education is actually provided. Home education therefore requires prior approval. [Government guidance, “Thuisonderwijs”](https://www.gobierno.aw/nl/leerplicht).

The 2020 policy specifies instructor competence through qualifications: at least MAVO, MBO, HAVO, VWO or equivalent for preschool/primary provision; MBO, higher professional or university education or equivalent for secondary provision. Medical suitability and a certificate permitting work with children are also checked. [Policy, section A.2, p.2](https://www.ea.aw/pages/wp-content/uploads/l/leerplicht/Beleid-bijzondere-omstandigheden-in-kader-van-leerplicht-2020-06.pdf#page=2).

### Educational approach

The policy explicitly states:

> De voor Land Aruba vastgestelde kerndoelen of eindtermen moeten dus geenszins gevolgd worden

The national core objectives or attainment targets need not be followed. Methods and content may differ substantially from school education. Inspection instead assesses breadth and full development, including linguistic, mathematical, social, cultural and motor development. This permits an interest-driven approach, subject to approval and these developmental requirements. [Policy, section A.2, p.2](https://www.ea.aw/pages/wp-content/uploads/l/leerplicht/Beleid-bijzondere-omstandigheden-in-kader-van-leerplicht-2020-06.pdf#page=2).

## Statistics

The Department of Public Health's youth-health report for 2021–July 2023 records **12 home-educated primary pupils aged 6–12** in its JZA registration system for school year 2022–2023, with a reference date of **31 May 2023**. This is a registration count for that age group, rather than an all-age national home-education total. [DVG/JGZ report, p.36](https://dvg.aw/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Officiele-Rapportage-Jeugdgezondheidszorg-JGZ-DVG-2021-Juli-2023.pdf#page=36).

The subsequent 2023–2024 report records 74 primary-sector schools and separately identifies a home-education group, without giving that group's pupil count in its registration overview. The school count is not a home-education enrolment figure. [DVG/JGZ annual report 2023–2024, p.35](https://dvg.aw/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Officiele-Jaarverslag-Jeugdgezondheidszorg-2023-2024-last-Version.pdf#page=35).
