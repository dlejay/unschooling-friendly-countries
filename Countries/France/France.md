# Unschooling in France

## Compulsory education

Since the 2019 *loi pour une école de la confiance* (*loi Blanquer*), instruction is compulsory from the beginning of the school year in the calendar year in which a child turns 3 until the age of 16.

## Home education

The *loi n° 2021-1109 du 24 août 2021 confortant le respect des principes de la République* replaced the previous declaration system with a system of prior authorisation from the 2022/23 school year. Home education is therefore no longer available solely on the basis of parental choice.

Authorisation may be granted only on one of four statutory grounds:

1. the child's health or disability;
2. intensive sporting or artistic activities;
3. family itinerancy within France or geographical remoteness from any public school; or
4. a situation specific to the child which motivates the educational project.

For the fourth ground, the parents must demonstrate that the proposed home education responds to a situation specific to the child. A preference for a particular pedagogical method is not, by itself, sufficient to establish such a situation.

As a general rule, an application must be submitted between 1 March and 31 May preceding the relevant school year. Applications may be made later where certain circumstances arise after this period, notably health, disability or geographical remoteness, and special provisions exist where a child's physical or moral integrity is threatened. Home education therefore cannot generally be commenced immediately as a matter of parental choice.

## Educational approach

France does not require a homeschooled child to follow the national school curriculum or the same pedagogical progression as a school pupil. The authorities must take account of the educational choices and pedagogical methods adopted by the family.

Interest-driven education is therefore possible as a pedagogical approach. However, it is not unrestricted: the child must make progressive and continuous acquisitions in every domain of the *socle commun de connaissances, de compétences et de culture*, with the objective of mastering the whole common foundation by the end of compulsory education. Progress must also be compatible with the child's age.

Where authorisation is sought on the basis of a situation specific to the child, the application must describe the pedagogical approach and methods, educational resources, and the organisation of the child's time, and explain how the child will acquire knowledge and skills in each domain of the common foundation.

Home-educated children are subject to pedagogical controls. The control assesses the reality of the instruction, the child's acquisitions and, subsequently, the child's progress, while taking account of the pedagogical methods chosen by the family.

## Statistics

| School year | Children authorised / instructed at home |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------: |
| 2025/26     |                                   28,810 |
| 2024/25     |                                   30,644 |
| 2023/24     |                                   47,802 |
| 2022/23     |                                   55,747 |
| 2021/22     |                                   72,369 |

The 2021/22 figure predates the authorisation regime. The 2022/23 and 2023/24 figures were affected by transitional provisions granting automatic authorisation to certain children who had already been home educated in 2021/22. That transitional regime ended after 2023/24.

For 2024/25, 30,644 authorisations had been granted from 40,846 applications as of 1 November 2024.

For 2025/26, the latest national figures published by the Ministry of Education record 28,810 authorisations from 36,309 applications, an authorisation rate of 79.3%, as of 1 November 2025.
