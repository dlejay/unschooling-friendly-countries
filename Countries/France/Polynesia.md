# Home education in French Polynesia

## Compulsory education

Article LP 2 of Law of the Country No.2017-15, as amended by No.2022-4, requires education from **3 to 16 years**. It begins with the school year in which the child turns three before 31 December. Education may be provided at school or within the family by parents or a person they choose. [Education Charter, LP 2, p.2](https://lexpol.cloud.pf/LexpolAfficheContenuPdf.php?texte=855&version=1772).

## Legal route and prior procedure

LP 2 lists medical care, disability pending placement in a medico-social establishment, sporting or artistic activities, itinerant parents and geographical remoteness. It also provides:

> Furthermore, any reason other than those listed […] must be specified at the time of the declaration.

Unofficial English translation of [LP 2, p.2; original French text](https://lexpol.cloud.pf/LexpolAfficheContenuPdf.php?texte=855&version=1772). No particular diploma is required of the person providing instruction. An annual declaration and the education ministry's prior opinion are required.

DGEE guidance requires a reason supported by documents, including for other grounds. The signed application records the child's identity, address and educational level and includes identity documents, proof of residence and, where applicable, the other parent's agreement and the instructor's identity. It is submitted through the official online portal. A declaration must also be made to the municipal mayor (Tavana). Declarations are due before the September holidays; later requests require justification and discussion with DGEE. A positive opinion leads to an attestation. The procedure is renewed each school year, and changes of educational choice are notified within eight days. [DGEE, procedures, section A and section C](https://www.education.pf/lorientation/j-inscris-mon-enfant-en-ief-ou-en-etab-prive-hors-contrat/).

## Educational standard and assessment

DGEE defines instruction as:

> […] acquiring the knowledge and skills of the common core throughout the child's schooling or home education.

Unofficial English translation of [DGEE, legal framework](https://www.education.pf/lorientation/j-inscris-mon-enfant-en-ief-ou-en-etab-prive-hors-contrat/).

LP 3 provides for municipal enquiries in the first year and every two years, examining the stated reasons, health compatibility and family circumstances. Inspectors assess teaching content, learning quality, conditions and achievement of objectives. Instruction at one address must concern children of one family. Required knowledge is governed by the applicable Polynesian texts. [Education Charter, LP 3, p.2](https://lexpol.cloud.pf/LexpolAfficheContenuPdf.php?texte=855&version=1772).

DGEE's pedagogical checks examine learning progress; parents must provide evidence of medical follow-up, and a report is sent to its director. [DGEE, controls, section B](https://www.education.pf/lorientation/j-inscris-mon-enfant-en-ief-ou-en-etab-prive-hors-contrat/).

## Official statistics

At the Assembly's education committee meeting on **18 January 2023**, the DGEE director reported **118 children receiving home education**. [Official committee record, p.21](https://www.assemblee.pf/travaux/downloadTexte/1592422).
